# Graded sample copies for TEF/TCF Canada and DELF B2 / DALF C1 written production

Scope note: researched 2026-10-27. FEI's site (france-education-international.fr) returned "ERR-BOT-403 / Accès refusé" to every automated fetch, so FEI pages were read only through search snippets or through official FEI/CIEP documents mirrored elsewhere (named below). All candidate texts are summarised; quotes are short phrases from the examiner comments.

## 1. Where are official sample copies with examiner comments published, and under what terms?

### Takeaway
Neither test maker publishes graded, commented copies for **TEF Canada** (CCI Paris) or **TCF Canada** (FEI). FEI even states that TCF copies are never annotated. The only official, commented, scored learner texts found are **DELF/DALF** copies produced or validated by the **CIEP** (FEI's former name): an ALTE/Council of Europe calibration pack (one copy each at A1, A2, B1, B2 and a C1 synthèse, about 2005) and an Ontario/CIEP pack (two B1 copies and one B2 copy, 2013). All of them were scored on the **old** analytic DELF grids, which were replaced around 2022 by banded grids (below / at / above target level). Their scores therefore don't map one-to-one onto today's grid, but their criterion-by-criterion comments are still valid calibration material.

### Cited Findings
**TEF Canada (CCI Paris Île-de-France, "Le français des affaires")**
- The official "Exemples de sujets – Expression écrite" PDF gives the task format, the criteria and 4 sample prompts per section, but **no candidate answers and no graded copies**. Section A: continue a faits-divers article, "80 mots minimum", "en faisant plusieurs paragraphes", 25 min. Section B: a letter to a newspaper reacting to a quoted statement, 200 words minimum, "Développez au moins 3 arguments", 35 min. Total 1 hour. Footer: "© Le français des affaires - CCI Paris Ile-de-France". — [CCI, tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf)
- Sample Section B statements: banning TV advertising; free access to culture; social networks as "un danger permanent pour les enfants"; package tours vs real discovery of a country. Section A openers: weddings on a volcano summit, an escaped lion, a Montreal resident finding treasure, an 80-year-old woman who catches car thieves. — [same PDF](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf)
- TEF scoring as described there: **two independent markers**, "spécialistes de la didactique des langues", using "une grille d'évaluation standardisée" that yields a CEFR level. They judge (a) text quality and effectiveness: "le respect de la situation", "l'organisation générale du texte", "la clarté de l'information ou de l'argumentation", "la capacité à développer, nuancer, préciser ses propos"; and (b) language: lexique, syntaxe (phrases, temps et modes), orthographe. The questions markers ask: is the text "clair, limpide, confus, incohérent?"; is the information "sommaires, vagues, précises, détaillées?"; are sentences "simples, complexes, maîtrisées?"; is the vocabulary "juste, précis, adéquat?". — [same PDF](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf)
- A separate CCI PDF covers the TEF IRN writing test, a different format (30 minutes for both sections). Don't mix it with TEF Canada. — [CCI, exemples-epreuves-tefirn-ee.pdf](https://lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/exemples-epreuves-tefirn-ee.pdf) (seen in search results only)

**TCF Canada (France Éducation international)**
- *Manuel du candidat*, Version H (February 2024), tasks for TCF TP/Québec/Canada. Tâche 1: a message to describe, narrate or explain, **60–120 words**. Tâche 2: an article, letter or note reporting an experience, with comments or arguments, **120–150 words**. Tâche 3: a text comparing **two points of view from two documents**, then giving one's own opinion, **120–180 words**. 60 minutes. — [Manuel du candidat TCF, Version H (mirrored by AF Madrid)](https://www.afmadrid.es/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Manuel_du_candidat_TCF.pdf)
- Marking: double-blind by FEI's central markers; "Chaque correcteur attribue à chacune des 3 tâches un niveau allant du niveau « A1 non atteint » au niveau « C2 »", and a calculation over the 6 levels gives the final mark out of 20. A third marking is done when the two markers differ widely. Criteria: linguistic (étendue du lexique, correction grammaticale, orthographe, degré d'élaboration des phrases), pragmatic (cohérence et cohésion, développement thématique), sociolinguistic (adéquation à la situation). — [Manuel du candidat](https://www.afmadrid.es/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Manuel_du_candidat_TCF.pdf)
- **No commented copies exist by policy:** "Les copies de l'épreuve d'expression écrite ne sont pas annotées… aucun élément matériel (et notamment pas de pistes d'amélioration) ne pourrait être transmis aux candidats." — [Manuel du candidat](https://www.afmadrid.es/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Manuel_du_candidat_TCF.pdf)
- TCF Canada writing → NCLC: 16–20 = NCLC 10+ (C1–C2); 14–15 = NCLC 9 (C1); 12–13 = NCLC 8 (B2); 10–11 = NCLC 7 (B2); 7–9 = NCLC 6 (B1); 6 = NCLC 5 (B1); 4–5 = NCLC 4 (A2). — [Manuel du candidat](https://www.afmadrid.es/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Manuel_du_candidat_TCF.pdf)
- FEI's public "Exemples d'épreuves du TCF" page (as indexed) shows comprehension items; the manual points to sample *subjects* (not copies) for EE at france-education-international.fr/document/tcf-exemple-epreuve-ee. — [FEI, Exemples d'épreuves du TCF](https://france-education-international.fr/test/exemples-epreuves-tcf?langue=fr); [Manuel du candidat](https://www.afmadrid.es/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Manuel_du_candidat_TCF.pdf)

### Inferences
- For TEF/TCF Canada calibration, the usable primary anchors are (a) the official criteria (TEF PDF, TCF manual) and (b) DELF B2 / DALF C1 commented copies and grids as proxies. TEF Section B (a 200+ word argued letter to a newspaper) is close to the DELF B2 task (a 250-word argued letter or contribution). TCF Tâche 3 (compare two views, then give your own) is a short mix of DALF C1 synthèse and essai.
- All official commented copies predate the current grids (≈2022). Use their comments as qualitative anchors ("what an examiner notices"), and re-band them on the new B2/B2+ and C1/C1+ descriptors instead of reusing their half-point scores.
- None of the licences found allow redistribution. The CCI PDF is ©; the CIEP and Ontario packs state no licence. For few-shot prompts, paraphrase the comments and use new or synthetic candidate texts rather than embedding the originals.

### Gaps
- FEI's own pages (DELF B2 "Exemples de sujets", "Grilles et critères", TCF EE sample) couldn't be fetched (bot block), so I couldn't confirm whether FEI now hosts any commented copies itself.
- No public TEF Canada graded copies, and the TEF marking grid itself (descriptors and bands) isn't public. CCI could be asked through tef@cci-paris-idf.fr.
- No official DALF C1 **essai** copy with comments was found; only the C1 synthèse copy. No official C2 copy.
- Reuse terms for the CIEP/ALTE pack and the Ontario pack aren't stated in the documents read.

## 2. Each sample found: task, score, and examiner comments by criterion

### Takeaway
Six official commented copies are usable (A1/A2 left out as irrelevant): **B1 24.5/25, B1 19/25, B1 17.5/25, B2 15.5/25, B2 15/25, C1 synthèse 9.5/13**. The two B2 copies score about 15/25 for the same reasons: generic arguments with few concrete details or examples, B1-level connectors, slips in register and letter conventions, and recurring verb-form errors. Neither is ever called incomprehensible.

### Cited Findings
**S1. DELF B2: formal letter to a company director asking for remote work (CIEP/ALTE pack, ≈2005)** — [EAQUALS PDF pp. 24–27](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf)
- Task: on behalf of colleagues with young children and short of office space, write to the director asking to work from home, with the benefits for the company. About 250 words, 1 hour.
- Score: **15/25** (communication 8.5/14, language 6.5/11).
- Task and argument: the writer puts the colleagues before the company ("profitable autant pour nous que pour l'entreprise"), and "oublie de mentionner l'argument qui doit motiver la requête", the lack of space. "L'argumentation est simple par rapport au niveau B2."
- Register: addresses the director "trop directe" ("Vous devriez seulement payer…" where "L'entreprise n'aurait plus qu'à prendre en charge…" was expected). Letter conventions not fully met: no date, a sign-off that is "trop courte et informelle", wrong punctuation after "M. le Directeur :".
- Length: **140 words only**, penalised under the respect of the instructions.
- Cohesion: few connectors; "Par contre" misused.
- Language: still B2 because there are no errors that cause misunderstanding, but "vie familial" (agreement) and "qui"/"qu'il" are errors "qui auraient dû être évitées"; some wrong word choices that don't block meaning; "Les phrases sont relativement simples pour ce niveau."

**S2. DELF B2: official letter to a school principal to set up a student free-expression space on the school website (Ontario/CIEP, 2013, 261 words)** — [PE_Exemple_6_B2.pdf](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_6_B2.pdf)
- Score: **15.5/25**.
- Respect de la consigne: right situation and genre; 261 words ≥ 250. Full marks implied.
- Sociolinguistic: a suitably formal opening and close, but the third paragraph is "un peu familier" (a rhetorical "à qui le demandez-vous ?"), and the reader no longer knows whether the director or the students are addressed: "une rupture dans le message".
- Presenting facts: "très peu de faits précis"; stays with "généralités" and never explains how the site would work or what would be posted.
- Argument: names some benefits (suggesting improvements, anonymity) and two key points, but "peu d'exemples et de détails pertinents". One idea (students voting directly on the site) "ne rassurera pas le directeur": an argument that works against the persuasive aim.
- Cohesion: 4 paragraphs is helpful, but the connectors (ensuite, lorsque, si) "relèvent davantage du niveau B1 que du niveau B2"; missing transitions mean the reader must "reconstituer le sens de certains passages".
- Vocabulary range: "plutôt étendue" (prendre connaissance, atteindre un objectif, résoudre…). Control: calques and imprecise collocations ("faire des décisions", "confortable", "offrir un commentaire", "éliminer les décisions").
- Spelling: most expected words correct.
- Grammar: infinitive vs past participle after prepositions and modals ("nous vous prions de reconsidéré", "pourront votés", "Veillez agréez"); past participles ("serait résoudre", "seraient fait"); elementary agreement ("tout l'école"); "et" for "est". Verdict: "n'est pas représentative d'un « bon contrôle grammatical »".
- Sentence complexity: real effort at varied complex sentences, but some structures are too oral, and future/conditional sequencing isn't controlled.

**S3. DELF B1: essay on the most important changes of the last 20 years in your country (CIEP/ALTE, ≈2005, 160–180 words)** — [EAQUALS PDF pp. 16–19](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf)
- Score: **17.5/25** (communication 9.5/13, language 8/12).
- Good: introduction, three advantages, two drawbacks, conclusion; ideas linked with "mais maintenant, et puis, bien sûr, comme".
- Lost points: confused sentences that "risque de poser problème au lecteur"; "La capacité à développer ses idées… est limitée."
- Language: vocabulary "relativement étendu", with a good use of a near-synonym ("l'invasion de la vie privée" for intrusion); impropriétés ("beaucoup de l'ordinateur"); pronoun misuse; a missing relative clause where one was needed.

**S4. DELF B1: reply to a survey on politeness among young people (Ontario/CIEP, 2013, 182 words)** — [PE_Exemple_4_B1.pdf](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_4_B1.pdf)
- Score: **24.5/25**, a near-ceiling B1 anchor.
- Rewarded: specific personal examples ("hier une fille m'a dit…", someone holding a door), varied opinion markers ("Je pense que…", "Pour moi, la politesse veut dire…"), a clear opening, positive then negative, and a conclusion. Repeating the topic words (poli, politesse) isn't penalised, since synonyms are hard to find. Many complétives; a range of tenses.
- Errors noted but tolerated at B1: "être traiter", ses/ces, "choses méchants", "leur vies".

**S5. DELF B1: same politeness survey (Ontario/CIEP, 2013, 179 words)** — [PE_Exemple_5_B1.pdf](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_5_B1.pdf)
- Score: **19/25**.
- Lost points: few facts; an example "repris du support et répété deux fois" (recycling the prompt); "manque de variété dans sa façon de présenter son opinion"; "les connecteurs sont peu variés"; "chose" used 4 times; spelling errors in common words; present tense only, where the marker "aurait attendu… le futur et le subjonctif, ne serait-ce qu'une hypothèse"; many elementary agreement and verb-ending errors "non attendues à ce niveau".

**S6. DALF C1: synthèse of 2 documents on Indigenous peoples vs animal-rights campaigns (seal and whale hunting; Yellowstone bison) (CIEP/ALTE, ≈2005, target 220 words, range 200–240)** — [EAQUALS PDF pp. 29–36](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf)
- Score: **9.5/13** (communication 5.5/7.5, language 4/5.5).
- Objectivity respected: no outside ideas, no personal comments.
- Content: most key points kept, but it misses the contradiction in the modern society that decides which species to protect (text 1) and that Indigenous people defend bison for cultural *and* food reasons (text 2). "Un peu court : 202 mots au lieu de 220."
- Cohesion: "L'exposé est linéaire : il suit l'ordre des textes… il n'y a pas de plan personnel"; "mais" misused.
- Lexis: good overall, but register slips ("des fois" for "parfois") and wrong word choices ("future" for "avenir").
- Grammar: three errors "qui ne devraient pas apparaître à ce niveau-là": "insistent que" (for "insistent pour que"), "cette dernier", "tous ce qu'ils peuvent". Varied phrasing credited ("Tel est le cas…", "Ayant vu…").

### Inferences
- Pattern across the official B2 copies: about 15/25 means "B2 language, B1 discourse". The vocabulary and accuracy mostly pass, but the argument is generic, the connectors are basic and the conventions of the genre are sloppy. A reviewer calibrated on these should score a 250-word text with no concrete examples and only *ensuite / par contre / si* at the low end of B2, even if it has few errors.
- Examiners judge errors by the level expected ("non attendues à ce niveau", "qui ne devraient pas apparaître à ce niveau-là"). The same error weighs more in a C1 copy than in a B1 copy. An AI reviewer should tag errors with the target level, not just list them.
- Examiners quote the text as evidence for every criterion. That format (criterion → verdict → quoted evidence) is a good template for few-shot outputs.

### Gaps
- No official copy at B2+ / C1 level for an argued essay or letter (the strong end), so the "strong copy" side of the comparison relies on the grid descriptors, not on examples.
- The candidate texts of S1, S3 and S6 are scanned images in the EAQUALS PDF and weren't extracted. Only the comments were read.

## 3. Recurring patterns: what separates an adequate copy from a strong one at B2 and C1

### Takeaway
The current official grids state the difference directly. **B2 (adequate):** a clear argument with for/against justifications and examples; a "gamme" of connectors; register adapted with rare slips; a wide but imperfect vocabulary; good control of common complex structures "peu variées". **B2+ (strong):** the problem and the position stated clearly, arguments "intégrés de façon élaborée" with the **reader's point of view** taken into account, "articulateurs variés", formality kept up "sur l'ensemble de la production", synonyms that get round gaps, varied phrasing. At C1 the step to C1+ is **developed arguments plus concrete examples**, being **convincing**, many strategies to involve the reader, and great flexibility of sentences.

### Cited Findings
**DELF B2 current grid (5 criteria × 0/1/3/5 = /25)** — [Grille_PE_B2.pdf](https://delfdalf.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/Nouvelles_Grilles/Grille_PE_B2.pdf)
- *Réalisation de la tâche.* Below: "expose simplement son opinion, ses goûts et ses préférences", uses concrete examples. **B2:** "argumentation claire… en apportant des justifications pour ou contre et en expliquant les avantages et inconvénients de différentes options", examples "en adéquation avec la situation". **B2+:** "présenter clairement la problématique, son opinion et les éventuelles propositions ou conclusions", "intégrer de façon élaborée divers arguments, justifications et exemples… en tenant compte du point de vue du destinataire".
- *Cohérence et cohésion.* Below: "quelques articulateurs de base"; layout and punctuation not always well used. **B2:** "en général bien organisé… une gamme de mots de liaison", minor errors allowed (e.g. "une même idée scindée en deux paragraphes"). **B2+:** "clair, fluide et bien structuré… articulateurs variés"; layout "logiques et facilitantes".
- *Adéquation sociolinguistique.* Below: common expressions, register "globalement adapté". **B2:** adapts to the reader and register; slips "rares et ne provoquent pas de tension pour le lecteur". **B2+:** adjusts formality "avec souplesse" and keeps it up throughout.
- *Lexique.* Below: simple vocabulary, limited range for "sujets de société courants", paraphrases; spelling errors many or below level. **B2:** "large gamme de vocabulaire", "varier sa formulation pour éviter les répétitions", some control errors. **B2+:** "vaste répertoire… surmonter facilement les lacunes par des synonymes ou des périphrases"; spelling correct except on complex words.
- *Morphosyntaxe.* Below: simple forms OK, struggles to stay accurate with more elaborate ideas. **B2:** "bon contrôle grammatical des structures syntaxiques complexes les plus fréquentes, bien que ces dernières soient peu variées"; errors that "ne conduisent pas à des malentendus". **B2+:** good control "et fait preuve de variété dans ses formulations".

**DALF C1 current grid (synthèse /12.5 + essai /12.5)** — [Grille_PE_C1.pdf](https://delfdalf.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/Nouvelles_Grilles/Grille_PE_C1.pdf)
- *Essai, réalisation de la tâche.* Below: "arguments… peu développés, confus ou manquent d'exemples", viewpoint "peu ou mal exprimé". **C1:** clear argument that "peut manquer ponctuellement de développement et / ou d'exemples concrets". **C1+:** "argumentation claire et fluide, étayée par des arguments développés et des exemples concrets… se montrer convaincant".
- *Essai, cohérence.* Below: "légères ruptures de sens", awkward connector choices. **C1:** "une grande variété d'articulateurs qui renforcent l'argumentation". **C1+:** the same "avec souplesse"; "clair, précis, fluide".
- *Essai, sociolinguistique.* Below: reader "pas clairement identifié ni / ou pris en compte". **C1:** "quelques expressions pour impliquer le destinataire". **C1+:** "nombreuses stratégies de communication pour interpeler et / ou produire un effet sur le destinataire".
- *Lexique at C1.* Below: frequent repetitions and errors "sur le lexique conceptuel". **C1:** "vaste répertoire lexical concret et abstrait". **C1+:** "élaboré… souplesse et précision".
- *Morphosyntaxe at C1.* Below: "manque de souplesse", recurring awkwardness on complex ideas. **C1:** high accuracy, some complex structures "répétitives ou maladroites". **C1+:** high accuracy throughout, "grande souplesse… structures variées".
- *Synthèse.* C1+ = a "plan personnel bien structuré et fluide qui croise efficacement les informations des textes"; below = "résumés juxtaposés"; objectivity (no outside elements) scored separately.

**What the commented copies actually point to**
- Concrete, situation-specific detail is credited; generalities are penalised (S2: "s'en tient à des généralités"; S4 rewarded for specific personal incidents). — [Ex. 6 B2](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_6_B2.pdf); [Ex. 4 B1](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_4_B1.pdf)
- Connectors are judged by level, not just by presence (S2: "relèvent davantage du niveau B1"). — [Ex. 6 B2](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_6_B2.pdf)
- Persuasion with the reader in mind: an argument that won't reassure the addressee is a flaw (S2), and putting the writer's interests before the addressee's is a flaw (S1). — [Ex. 6 B2](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_6_B2.pdf); [EAQUALS PDF](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf)
- At C1, structure means a **personal plan** that crosses sources, not following the order of the documents (S6). — [EAQUALS PDF](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf)
- Varied tenses and moods are expected (S5: wanted a future, a subjunctive, "ne serait-ce qu'une hypothèse"). — [Ex. 5 B1](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_5_B1.pdf)

### Inferences
- For an AI reviewer, the most discriminating B2 vs B2+ signals to check are: (1) a stated problem plus position plus conclusion or proposal; (2) each argument backed by a concrete, situation-fitting example; (3) explicit attention to the reader's objections or interests; (4) connector variety beyond *et / mais / ensuite / par contre*; (5) register kept up throughout, including the letter conventions (TEF Section B is a letter to a newspaper); (6) synonyms instead of repetition; (7) varied complex structures (relatives, concessives, hypotheticals, subjunctive) with few errors.
- Error counts alone don't separate B2 from C1 in these grids. Development, examples, persuasion and flexibility do. That matches Ink's "precision over recall" stance: flagging errors is only one of five criteria.

### Gaps
- No official TEF or TCF descriptors per band, so the B2/C1 distinction for TEF/TCF is inferred from the DELF/DALF grids and the CEFR.
- No official strong (B2+/C1+) argued copy found to illustrate the top band.

## 4. Common reasons copies lose points

### Takeaway
Officially documented causes of lost points: **under length** (fatal below 50% in DELF/DALF, and grounds for "A1 non atteint" in TCF), **off-topic** (thematic or discursive), **tasks left out**, **register and genre-convention slips**, **generic arguments without concrete examples**, **lists of points without links or development**, **recycling the prompt**, **copying source text** (synthèse), **linear summaries instead of a personal plan**, **basic or misused connectors**, **repetition**, and **elementary errors below the target level**. Memorised formulas aren't named in any official document found.

### Cited Findings
- **Length and missing tasks (TCF):** a copy "pourrait être évaluée « A1 non atteint »" if "chaque tâche n'est pas réalisée avec le nombre de mots exigé", if it is "hors-sujet", if tasks aren't done, or if it is illegible. — [TCF Manuel](https://www.afmadrid.es/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Manuel_du_candidat_TCF.pdf)
- **Length (DELF B2 current):** under 50% of the expected length ("124 mots ou moins") → 0 on every criterion. — [Grille_PE_B2](https://delfdalf.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/Nouvelles_Grilles/Grille_PE_B2.pdf). **DALF C1 synthèse:** ≤ 99 words → 0; 300+ words → can't be rated C1 for the task; outside 200–240 loses the 0.5 length point. — [Grille_PE_C1](https://delfdalf.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/Nouvelles_Grilles/Grille_PE_C1.pdf). The **old** C1 synthèse rule was −1 point per 10% outside the range. — [EAQUALS PDF](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf). In practice, the S1 B2 copy at 140 words was marked down. — [EAQUALS PDF](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf)
- **Off-topic (DELF B2 / DALF C1 current):** thematic → no "+" band for task and lexis; discursive (wrong genre, e.g. an essay instead of a letter) → can't reach B2 for task and coherence; complete → 0 for task, coherence and sociolinguistic. — [Grille_PE_B2](https://delfdalf.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/Nouvelles_Grilles/Grille_PE_B2.pdf); [Grille_PE_C1](https://delfdalf.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/Nouvelles_Grilles/Grille_PE_C1.pdf)
- **Copying the documents (C1 synthèse):** reusing whole passages for more than ¾ of the text → 0 for lexis and grammar. — [Grille_PE_C1](https://delfdalf.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/Nouvelles_Grilles/Grille_PE_C1.pdf)
- **Recycling the prompt's examples** (S5: an example "repris du support et répété deux fois"). — [Ex. 5 B1](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_5_B1.pdf)
- **Generic, undeveloped arguments** (S1 "argumentation… simple"; S2 "généralités", "peu d'exemples"; S3 "capacité à développer… limitée"); the C1 grid's "below" band: "arguments… peu développés… manquent d'exemples". — [EAQUALS PDF](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf); [Ex. 6 B2](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_6_B2.pdf); [Grille_PE_C1](https://delfdalf.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/Nouvelles_Grilles/Grille_PE_C1.pdf)
- **Leaving out the key argument the prompt implies** (S1 never mentions the lack of space). — [EAQUALS PDF](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf)
- **Register and genre conventions:** tone too direct or familiar toward a superior; missing date; casual sign-off; addressee unclear partway through. — [EAQUALS PDF](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf); [Ex. 6 B2](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_6_B2.pdf). Register slips in vocabulary at C1 ("des fois"). — [EAQUALS PDF](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf)
- **Coherence:** missing transitions, so the reader "reconstitue le sens"; connectors below level or misused ("Par contre", "mais"); linear structure that follows the sources. — [Ex. 6 B2](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_6_B2.pdf); [EAQUALS PDF](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf)
- **Lexis:** calques and wrong collocations ("faire des décisions", "offrir un commentaire"), repetition ("chose" ×4), the wrong word in a near pair ("future"/"avenir"). — [Ex. 6 B2](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_6_B2.pdf); [Ex. 5 B1](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_5_B1.pdf); [EAQUALS PDF](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf)
- **Grammar errors examiners single out as below level:** -er infinitive vs past participle (-é/-er after prepositions and modals); past-participle agreement in passives; gender agreement (tout l'école, cette dernier); est/et; qui/qu'il; verb government (insister pour que); tense sequencing (future vs conditional); using only the present tense. — [Ex. 6 B2](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_6_B2.pdf); [EAQUALS PDF](https://www.eaquals.org/wp-content/uploads/French-samples-CIEP.pdf); [Ex. 5 B1](https://transformingfsl.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/PE_Exemple_5_B1.pdf)
- **TEF-specific instructions that set up these failures:** Section A "en faisant plusieurs paragraphes" and Section B "au moins 3 arguments", so a single block of text or fewer than 3 developed arguments fails the stated task. — [CCI TEF PDF](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf)
- Prep-site claim (non-official): DELF B2 tolerates about 10% under length (≈225 words). Not confirmed by the official grids read. — [Global Exam DELF B2 PE](https://global-exam.com/blog/fr/delf-b2-reussir-la-section-production-ecrite/) (seen in search results only)

### Inferences
- A TEF/TCF reviewer should check task compliance (word counts per task; paragraphs in TEF A; 3+ arguments and letter form in TEF B; both viewpoints summarised before one's own in TCF T3) before any language feedback, since these failures cap or zero the score whatever the accuracy.
- "Memorised formulas" and "lists of ideas" appear in official material only indirectly: "résumés juxtaposés", connectors judged by level, and "argumentation… simple". Prep sites' claims that examiners penalise canned openings should be marked as unverified.

### Gaps
- No official statement found on memorised or template phrases (e.g. canned openings) in TEF/TCF/DELF marking.
- No official TEF penalty rules for being under the 80/200-word minimums, or for missing paragraphs or arguments, beyond the instructions themselves.
- No official statement on over-length penalties for TCF (it has maxima of 120/150/180) other than the "nombre de mots exigé" clause.
