# TCF Canada, Expression écrite: format, official criteria, scoring, NCLC mapping (as of October 2026)

**Read this first.** france-education-international.fr (FEI) returns an anti-bot "Accès refusé / ERR-BOT-403" page to the fetch tool, so I could not read any FEI page in full. Everything marked "FEI" below comes from the search engine's extracts of FEI pages (the URLs are given). These are close to verbatim, but they are not a full reading. canada.ca, France Compétences and the prep sites were read in full. Labels: **[OFFICIAL]** means FEI, IRCC or France Compétences. **[PREP, non-official]** means it comes only from prep or test-centre sites.

## 1. Task format: text types, audience, word ranges, time, instructions, word-count rules

### Takeaway
The test is 60 minutes for three tasks, written in order. Tâche 1 is a 60–120-word message to named recipients. Tâche 2 is a 120–150-word article, letter or note giving an account of an experience, with comments or arguments. Tâche 3 is a 120–180-word text that compares two viewpoints on a social issue, taken from two short documents, then gives the writer's own opinion. Officially, missing the minimum word count, writing off-topic or leaving a task undone can get the whole paper rated "A1 non atteint". No official source found says what happens above the maximum.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL] Duration and structure:** one collective test of 60 minutes with 3 tasks. — [FEI, TCF Canada page](https://www.france-education-international.fr/test/tcf-canada?langue=fr); also "épreuve de 60 minutes, composée de 3 tâches" (TCF tout public) — [France Compétences RS6460](https://www.francecompetences.fr/recherche/rs/6460/)
- **[OFFICIAL] Tâche 1:** "un message pour décrire, raconter ou expliquer" to one or more recipients; the recipients' status is set in the instructions. "Nombre de mots attendu : minimum 60 mots/maximum 120 mots." — [FEI, TCF Canada page](https://france-education-international.fr/test/tcf-canada) (search extract)
- **[OFFICIAL] Tâche 2:** a text for several recipients ("article, courrier, note…") giving a "compte rendu d'expérience ou un récit", with comments or arguments. "minimum 120 mots/maximum 150 mots." — [FEI, TCF Canada page](https://www.france-education-international.fr/test/tcf-canada?langue=fr) (search extract)
- **[OFFICIAL] Tâche 3:** a text, written for a newspaper, a website or a colleague, that compares two points of view on a social issue ("fait de société") expressed in two documents. The candidate also gives their own opinion on the topic. "minimum 120 mots/maximum 180 mots." — [FEI, TCF Canada page](https://www.france-education-international.fr/es/test/tcf-canada?langue=fr) (search extract). The "two documents" wording was clearly attached to the [FEI TCF Québec page](https://france-education-international.fr/es/test/tcf-quebec?langue=fr) in one extract. The TCF Canada extract gives the same task and the same 120–180 range.
- **[OFFICIAL] Generic TCF task objectives** (TCF tout public, the same three-task test): T1 "rédigent un message pour décrire un évènement ou un projet en réaction à un document déclencheur"; T2 "rédigent un récit d'activités en fonction d'un objectif précis"; T3 "expriment un point de vue argumenté en réaction à deux documents déclencheurs." — [France Compétences RS6460](https://www.francecompetences.fr/recherche/rs/6460/)
- **[OFFICIAL] How words are counted:** a word is "tout ensemble de signes placé entre deux espaces" (any string of characters between two spaces). — [FEI TCF Canada page](https://france-education-international.fr/test/tcf-canada) (search extract)
- **[OFFICIAL] Grounds for "A1 non atteint":** FEI warns that a paper can be rated "A1 non atteint" if a task is not done with the required number of words ("chaque tâche n'est pas réalisée avec le nombre mots exigé"), if it is off-topic ("hors sujet"), if a task is not done, or (on paper) if it is unreadable. — [FEI TCF Canada page](https://www.france-education-international.fr/test/tcf-canada?langue=fr) (search extract)
- **[OFFICIAL, caution] Sample tests:** FEI's demo document [tcf-tp-qc-ca-exemple-epreuve-ee](https://france-education-international.fr/document/tcf-tp-qc-ca-exemple-epreuve-ee) is the sample written test for TCF tout public, Québec and Canada. A different FEI document, [tcf-exemple-epreuve-ee](https://france-education-international.fr/document/tcf-exemple-epreuve-ee), is an older generic version with ranges of 30–60 and 40–90 words, which do not apply to TCF Canada (per a search summary; not read directly).
- **[PREP, reproducing FEI "sujets zéro"] Two-part split in tâche 3:** "dans la première partie, vous présentez les deux opinions avec vos propres mots (entre 40 et 60 mots)", then "dans la deuxième partie, vous donnez votre position" (80–120 words). — [GlobalExam](https://global-exam.com/blog/fr/exemple-dexpression-ecrite-du-tcf-et-corrige/), which credits the sample to FEI. The FEI description itself gives no split; treat 40–60 / 80–120 as what the sample prompt says, possibly varying by session.
- **[PREP] Task 1 wording:** "rédiger un message pour décrire, raconter quelque chose à une autre personne ou groupe de personnes"; **Task 2:** "sous forme de courrier, de note, d'article ou autre un compte rendu suite à une expérience vécue"; **Task 3:** "présente un échange de points de vue… prendre position et d'argumenter." — [GlobalExam](https://global-exam.com/blog/fr/exemple-dexpression-ecrite-du-tcf-et-corrige/)
- **[PREP] Other rules claimed:** no rough draft allowed ("Vous n'avez pas droit à un brouillon"); doing the tasks out of order makes the paper "automatiquement classée dans le niveau A1"; below the word minimum the task "sera obligatoirement notée « A1 non atteint »". No penalty is given for going over the maximum. — [GlobalExam](https://global-exam.com/blog/fr/exemple-dexpression-ecrite-du-tcf-et-corrige/)
- **[PREP, unverified] Penalties above the maximum:** one study guide claims that "text beyond 120 words is ignored by evaluator" (Task 1), that Task 1 at 50 words is "capped at A2", and that Task 3 at 110 words "cannot achieve B2/C1". It cites no FEI document, and its disclaimer says it has no affiliation with any exam sponsor. — [open-exam-prep](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/tcf-canada/written-production/ee-rubric-precision). These claims conflict with FEI's own rule (below the minimum → "A1 non atteint", not a cap at A2).

### Inferences
- For a reviewer: treat the minimum as a hard gate. Below it, FEI says the paper can be rated A1 non atteint, so warn at the very top. Treat the maximum as a soft limit: no official penalty is published, but writers should stay inside it. FEI calls it the "expected" range, and correctors may disregard text beyond it (a prep-site claim only).
- Count words FEI's way: split on spaces, so "l'homme" is one word and "c'est-à-dire" is one word.
- Tâche 3 is not a free essay. The writer has to restate both documents' positions in their own words (the sample prompt says "avec vos propres mots"), then take a side. The structure itself is part of task completion.
- Audience and register are set by each prompt (the recipients' status in T1; a newspaper, website or colleague in T3). The reviewer should read the prompt and check the register against it, not assume one.

### Gaps
- Could not read the full FEI TCF Canada page, the "presentationtcfcanada" document, or the sample-test PDFs (FEI blocks the fetcher). The exact candidate-facing instruction text per task is therefore unconfirmed.
- No official source on going over the maximum.
- No official source on whether the 40–60 / 80–120 split in tâche 3 appears in every session.
- Recommended time per task is not official. Prep sites suggest about 10, 20 and 30 minutes; one gives "25 minutes" for T3. None of these split figures could be verified.

## 2. Official evaluation criteria, grading process, and how the three tasks combine into /20

### Takeaway
FEI publishes the criterion families for writing, but not a public descriptor grid. The families: linguistic criteria (range of vocabulary, grammatical accuracy, spelling, how elaborate the sentences are); pragmatic criteria (coherence and cohesion, development of the topic); and sociolinguistic criteria (fit with the communicative situation). Papers are double-marked blind. Since May 2024, a computer-based paper gets one human mark and one automated mark (FIDELIA), with a third human marker when they disagree. How the three task levels become a single /20 is not published.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL] Double marking:** "Les épreuves d'expression écrite sont évaluées 2 fois de manière totalement indépendante et en double aveugle", by FEI's team of correctors. Correctors are chosen for "expérience notable dans le domaine du FLE et de l'évaluation", pass a selection test, and are trained and monitored by FEI. — [FEI, Évaluation des épreuves du TCF](https://www.france-education-international.fr/article/evaluation-epreuves-tcf?langue=fr) (search extract)
- **[OFFICIAL] Writing criteria:** linguistic ("étendue du lexique, correction grammaticale, orthographe, degré d'élaboration des phrases..."). In one extract of the same page, the writing list also includes pragmatic criteria (cohérence et cohésion, développement thématique) and sociolinguistic criteria (adéquation à la situation de communication). — [FEI, Évaluation des épreuves du TCF](https://www.france-education-international.fr/article/evaluation-epreuves-tcf?langue=es) (search extract). **Conflict:** a second extract of the page placed the pragmatic and sociolinguistic families only under the speaking test. For speaking, its pragmatic list reads "interaction, structuration, cohérence et cohésion". I could not settle this without a full read. All three families are standard CEFR-based dimensions, so their use for writing is very likely, but not confirmed verbatim.
- **[OFFICIAL] FIDELIA (change, May 2024):** "À partir de mai 2024, l'évaluation des productions d'expression écrite passées sur ordinateur associe une correction humaine et une correction par FIDELIA." The final level is computed from both marks. "En cas de désaccord, une troisième correction, humaine, sera assurée." According to FEI, the two marks agree in about 70% of cases. FIDELIA was developed with the CENTAL lab at UCLouvain. — [FEI, FIDELIA](https://www.france-education-international.fr/article/fidelia-la-solution-daide-la-correction-automatisee) (search extract)
- **[OFFICIAL] Who marks:** expression tests are marked by "examinateurs habilités dans les centres de passation, et par des correcteurs, intervenants extérieurs sélectionnés et formés par France Éducation International." — [France Compétences RS6460](https://www.francecompetences.fr/recherche/rs/6460/)
- **[OFFICIAL] Score format:** results for TCF Canada are given as a score out of 20 for written expression, translated into CEFR levels from "A1 non atteint" to C2. — [FEI, Comprendre mes résultats au TCF](https://www.france-education-international.fr/article/comprendre-mes-resultats-au-tcf) (search extract)
- **[OFFICIAL, research] NCLC alignment study:** CIEP (now FEI) aligned TCF written and spoken productions to the NCLC and CEFR in 2015, using the CEFR levels correctors gave a sample of productions, a panel that assigned NCLC levels, psychometric calibration, and a qualitative mapping of descriptors. Published by V. Folny, *CJAL* 23(2), 2020, pp. 20–72. — [Érudit abstract](https://www.erudit.org/fr/revues/cjal/2020-v23-n2-cjal05614/1072969ar/) (full PDF returned 403)
- **[PREP] Aggregation:** "Each corrector assigns a level from A1 to C2 to each task", the levels are averaged, and a third corrector steps in "Si un écart trop important est relevé". — [GlobalExam](https://global-exam.com/blog/fr/exemple-dexpression-ecrite-du-tcf-et-corrige/)
- **[PREP, unverified] A four-criterion rubric:** "Adéquation à la consigne", "Cohérence et cohésion", "Correction grammaticale", "Richesse et précision lexicales", called "an official 4-pillar rubric standard set by France Éducation International", with no FEI document cited and no weights. — [open-exam-prep](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/tcf-canada/written-production/ee-rubric-precision)
- **[PREP] Scoring method not public:** official documents publish the score-to-level conversion but not the calculation formula. — [Digischool](https://www.digischool.fr/articles/fle/comment-est-note-le-tcf-grille-points/)

### Inferences
- A reviewer's instructions can safely build on these dimensions:
  1. Task completion and fit with the communicative situation: purpose, recipient, register, every point the prompt asks for, the word range.
  2. Coherence and cohesion, and how far the topic is developed: paragraphing, connectors, progression.
  3. Range of vocabulary.
  4. Grammatical accuracy and how elaborate the sentences are.
  5. Spelling.

  These match FEI's published families and the CEFR written-production scales. Only (1)–(5) as families are official, not any per-level descriptor text.
- Computer-based papers since May 2024 are partly machine-scored (FIDELIA). Surface accuracy, length and sentence complexity are likely to weigh in consistently. This is an inference: FEI does not publish FIDELIA's features.
- Each task seems to be rated at a CEFR level and then combined (a prep-site claim). Tâche 3 likely shows the highest levels (B2–C1), so a weak T1 caps less than a weak T3 does. This last point is speculation, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No public official descriptor grid (per-level wording) for TCF writing was found. FEI may give one only to its correctors.
- How the three tasks are weighted and combined into /20 is not published.
- I could not resolve whether FEI's page lists pragmatic and sociolinguistic criteria under writing.

## 3. Mapping the /20 written expression score to NCLC/CLB and CEFR

### Takeaway
IRCC's current TCF Canada table (a single table, with no date bands; page dated 2026-05-28) gives, for Expression écrite: NCLC 10 = 16–20, 9 = 14–15, 8 = 12–13, 7 = 10–11, 6 = 7–9, 5 = 6, 4 = 4–5. The official CEFR conversion of a /20 score, published for the TCF on France Compétences, is: 0 A1 non atteint, 1 A1, 2–5 A2, 6–9 B1, 10–13 B2, 14–17 C1, 18–20 C2.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL] IRCC table, TCF Canada** (Compréhension de l'écrit / Expression écrite / Compréhension de l'oral / Expression orale):

  | NCLC | CE | **EE** | CO | EO |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 10 | 549–699 | **16–20** | 549–699 | 16–20 |
  | 9 | 524–548 | **14–15** | 523–548 | 14–15 |
  | 8 | 499–523 | **12–13** | 503–522 | 12–13 |
  | 7 | 453–498 | **10–11** | 458–502 | 10–11 |
  | 6 | 406–452 | **7–9** | 398–457 | 7–9 |
  | 5 | 375–405 | **6** | 369–397 | 6 |
  | 4 | 342–374 | **4–5** | 331–368 | 4–5 |

  The table has no date-based sub-tables, unlike TEF Canada's three (after 10 Dec 2023; 1 Oct 2019 to 10 Dec 2023; before 30 Sept 2019). Results must be "moins de 2 ans" old. Page details date: 2026-05-28. — [IRCC, Atlantic Immigration Program language page](https://www.canada.ca/fr/immigration-refugies-citoyennete/services/immigrer-canada/programme-immigration-atlantique/evaluation-competences-linquistiques.html). The same table is reproduced by [Digischool](https://www.digischool.fr/articles/fle/tcf-canada-entree-express/). The central IRCC "test equivalency charts" URL returned 404 to the fetcher.
- **[OFFICIAL] CEFR conversion of a /20 score:** "0/20 : niveau A1 non atteint; 1/20 : niveau A1; de 2 à 5/20 : niveau A2; de 6 à 9/20 : niveau B1; de 10 à 13/20 : niveau B2; de 14 à 17/20 : niveau C1; du 18 à 20/20 : niveau C2." — [France Compétences RS6460 (TCF tout public)](https://www.francecompetences.fr/recherche/rs/6460/); the same table appears in [Digischool](https://www.digischool.fr/articles/fle/comment-est-note-le-tcf-grille-points/). FEI says TCF Canada's /20 is "traduite en niveaux du CECRL" ([FEI](https://www.france-education-international.fr/article/comprendre-mes-resultats-au-tcf)), but I could not read FEI's own table to confirm that it is identical for TCF Canada.
- **[PREP] Context:** Digischool notes there is no official CEFR equivalent for the NCLC levels, and that 10/20 is "already the bottom of B2", not "a pass mark". NCLC 7 (10/20) is the common target for Express Entry. — [Digischool, TCF Canada et Entrée express](https://www.digischool.fr/articles/fle/tcf-canada-entree-express/)

### Inferences
- Combining the two tables: NCLC 7 (10–11) is low B2, NCLC 9 (14–15) is low C1, and NCLC 10 (16–20) spans upper C1 and C2. A reviewer aiming at NCLC 7 should coach to a solid B2 across all three tasks.

### Gaps
- No official FEI–IRCC table directly maps CEFR to NCLC for TCF Canada; the combination above is derived.
- Could not confirm on FEI's own site that TCF Canada uses exactly the RS6460 CEFR table.

## 4. What examiners penalise

### Takeaway
Officially penalised, up to "A1 non atteint": a task under its word minimum, off-topic writing, a task left undone, and (on paper) unreadable writing. The official criteria also reward fitting the communicative situation (register), coherence and cohesion, and developing the topic. Copying the documents in tâche 3 is not mentioned officially, but the sample prompt asks for the two opinions "avec vos propres mots". Every other penalty is a prep-site claim.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL]** "A1 non atteint" for a task not done with the required number of words, off-topic, task not done, or unreadable. — [FEI TCF Canada page](https://www.france-education-international.fr/test/tcf-canada?langue=fr) (search extract)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Sociolinguistic criterion: "adéquation à la situation de communication"; pragmatic: "cohérence et cohésion, développement thématique". — [FEI, Évaluation des épreuves du TCF](https://www.france-education-international.fr/article/evaluation-epreuves-tcf?langue=es) (search extract; see the conflict noted in §2)
- **[PREP, reproducing the FEI sample]** Tâche 3, part 1: present the two opinions "avec vos propres mots". — [GlobalExam](https://global-exam.com/blog/fr/exemple-dexpression-ecrite-du-tcf-et-corrige/)
- **[PREP]** Tasks done out of order put the paper in A1. — [GlobalExam](https://global-exam.com/blog/fr/exemple-dexpression-ecrite-du-tcf-et-corrige/)
- **[PREP]** Register: tu for a friend or family, vous for a director or organisation; mixing registers in one message is "one of the most commonly flagged Tâche 1 errors"; T3 should be formal. — [open-exam-prep T1](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/tcf-canada/written-production/ee-task-1-short-message), [open-exam-prep rubric](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/tcf-canada/written-production/ee-rubric-precision)

### Inferences
- Checks a reviewer should run:
  - Word count per task against its range (a hard error below the minimum, a warning above the maximum).
  - Every point the prompt asks for is covered (e.g. T1 asks to describe, explain or invite; T2 gives an account plus comments).
  - The register matches the recipient and stays the same throughout.
  - The text form matches the asked format (a message with greeting and sign-off; an article or letter).
  - In T3, both positions are restated in the writer's own words (lifting phrases from the documents probably adds nothing to the lexical score, and may be read as not doing that part of the task), then a clear personal stance with arguments.
  - Connectors and paragraphing.
  - Then lexis, grammar and spelling, with precision over recall.

### Gaps
- No official statement on copying or lifting from the T3 documents, or on how heavily a wrong register is penalised.

## 5. Changes to TCF Canada, 2023–2026

### Takeaway
The task format and word ranges seem unchanged in 2023–2026. The changes are to marking and procedure: FIDELIA machine co-marking for computer-based writing since May 2024, and re-marking requests suspended for about a year from the 1 September 2026 sessions. The IRCC NCLC table for TCF Canada has no date-based versions, unlike TEF Canada's December 2023 change.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL] May 2024:** computer-based written productions are marked by a human plus FIDELIA, with a third human marker on disagreement. — [FEI, FIDELIA](https://www.france-education-international.fr/article/fidelia-la-solution-daide-la-correction-automatisee) (search extract)
- **[OFFICIAL] 1 Sept 2026:** "À partir des sessions du TCF du 1er septembre 2026, les demandes de recorrection des épreuves d'expression orale et écrite ne seront plus acceptées pour une période d'environ 1 an." — [FEI, Évaluation des épreuves du TCF](https://www.france-education-international.fr/article/evaluation-epreuves-tcf?langue=fr) (search extract). Confirmed by a test centre: re-correction and "consultation de copie" are suspended. Complaints about exam conditions must be made on the day of the exam; complaints made after results are not admissible (notice dated 5 Sept 2026, updated 9 Sept 2026). — [Alliance Française de Delhi](https://afdelhi.org/?p=94417)
- **[PREP] Before Sept 2026:** re-correction was possible for the expression tests only, requested within one month of the results, and the new score could be lower. — [AF Delhi](https://afdelhi.org/?p=25649) (search summary)
- **[OFFICIAL] IRCC:** the TCF Canada table has no date-based versions; TEF Canada's changed for tests after 10 Dec 2023. Page dated 2026-05-28. — [IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/fr/immigration-refugies-citoyennete/services/immigrer-canada/programme-immigration-atlantique/evaluation-competences-linquistiques.html)
- **[PREP] Format unchanged:** sources from 2026 still describe 3 exercises in 60 minutes. — [Digischool](https://www.digischool.fr/articles/fle/tcf-canada-entree-express/), [Intercariforef](https://www.intercariforef.org/dian/print/03_251309237F/03_2534730S/2534730S/TCF%20Canada) (search summary)
- **[OFFICIAL, via prep] Volume:** in 2025, FEI reported 124,667 TCF Canada candidates (310,473 for the TCF overall), published 3 March 2026. — [Digischool](https://www.digischool.fr/articles/fle/tcf-canada-entree-express/)

### Inferences
- With re-marking suspended until about September 2027, a candidate's first result is final, which raises the stakes on meeting the word ranges and the task requirements.

### Gaps
- Could not check FEI's news pages for other 2025–2026 format changes; none were found in any source.
