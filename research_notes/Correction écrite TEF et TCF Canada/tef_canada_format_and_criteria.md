# TEF Canada: Expression écrite. Task format, official criteria, score scales, penalties, recent changes

Researched 2026-10-27. "OFFICIAL" means a document published by CCI Paris Île-de-France / Le français des affaires (FDA), which designs the TEF, or by IRCC. "PREP SITE" means a third-party claim with no official confirmation. Official PDFs were downloaded and their text extracted locally, so the quotes below are verbatim from the PDFs.

## 1. Section A and Section B: exact task format, word counts, timing, instructions

### Takeaway
OFFICIAL: the test lasts 60 minutes in two separately timed sections. **Section A** (25 min, **80 words minimum**): the candidate is given the start of a news article (*fait divers*) and has to finish it "in several paragraphs". **Section B** (35 min, **200 words minimum**): the candidate is given one sentence quoted from a newspaper and writes **a letter to the newspaper** giving their opinion, with **at least 3 arguments**. No maximum word count is published. Since 2025 the test is taken on computer in FDA's "Paragraphe" application. Once Section A is ended early, the candidate can't go back to it.

### Cited Findings
- Official presentation (FDA sample sheet, uploaded 2024-10): "Durée : 1 heure (durée limitée par section) / 2 activités : section A et section B". — [FDA, *Épreuve d'expression écrite, exemples de sujets*](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf) (also mirrored at [tefcanada.ca, 2025-06](https://tefcanada.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf))
- **Section A**, verbatim: "Type de document : le début d'un article de presse (rubrique faits-divers) / Objectif : écrire la suite de l'article (80 mots minimum) / Durée : 25 minutes". Consigne-type: "Voici le début d'un article de presse. → Terminez cet article : - en ajoutant à la suite un texte de 80 mots minimum ; - en faisant plusieurs paragraphes." — [FDA sample sheet](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf)
  - So yes, **the start of the text is given**: a headline plus 1–3 sentences, sometimes ending in "…". Official examples: "Mariages au sommet" (Arequipa, Peru: twenty couples climbed a 5,825 m volcano to marry); "Un lion sème la panique" (a zookeeper notices at nightfall that the lion is missing, then hears screams…); "Un nouveau riche" (a Montreal resident digging in his garden makes "une très agréable découverte…"); "Une mamie en colère" (an 80-year-old catches two men stealing her car and decides "de leur donner une bonne leçon"). — [same](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf)
- **Section B**, verbatim: "Type de document : une phrase extraite d'un journal / Objectif : exprimer son point de vue et le justifier (200 mots minimum) / Durée : 35 minutes". Consigne-type: "Vous avez lu dans un journal l'affirmation suivante : « Phrase issue d'un article de journal » → Écrivez une lettre au journal pour dire ce que vous en pensez (200 mots minimum). → Développez au moins 3 arguments pour défendre votre point de vue." — [same](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf)
  - Official example statements: « Il faut d'urgence supprimer la publicité à la télévision. »; « L'accès à la culture (cinéma, théâtre, expositions…) devrait être gratuit. »; « Les réseaux sociaux représentent un danger permanent pour les enfants. »; « Les voyages organisés sont incompatibles avec la découverte réelle d'un pays. » — [same](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf)
  - **Audience:** the newspaper, so readers of the press and the editor: a formal public letter. The official text says "lettre au journal" and nothing more about register.
- The skill each section tests, as the 2025 invigilator booklet names it: "Section A : Activité d'évaluation de la capacité à Transmettre des informations : « épreuve du fait divers »" and "Section B : Activité d'évaluation de la capacité à exprimer et justifier son point de vue « épreuve de la lettre »". The same booklet repeats "Section A : 80 mots minimum (25 minutes) / Section B : 200 mots minimum (35 minutes)" for TEF, TEF Canada and TEFAQ. — [FDA, *Livret du surveillant TEF* 2025](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-livret-du-surveillanttef.pdf)
- No going back: "Si le candidat clique sur « Terminer » avant la fin du temps imparti de la section A, il ne pourra plus revenir dessus et passera directement à la section B." — [Livret du surveillant 2025](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-livret-du-surveillanttef.pdf)
- Place in the test: TEF Canada has 4 compulsory tests: Compréhension écrite (40 questions, 60 min), Compréhension orale (40 questions, 40 min), Expression écrite (2 sujets, 60 min), Expression orale (2 sujets, 15 min). — [FDA, *Livret du responsable TEF* 2026 (uploaded 2026-04)](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2026-livret-du-responsable-tef.pdf). The FDA TEF Canada page adds: "Toutes les épreuves doivent être passées le même jour". — [FDA TEF Canada page](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/tests-diplomes/test-evaluation-francais-tef/tef-canada/)
  - Note: a search summary read the handbook's "2 sujets" as "2 sujets au choix". The handbook doesn't say "au choix", and the candidate handbook says "Les deux sections, A et B, sont à traiter." There is **no choice of topic**. — [Manuel du candidat e-TEF Canada (Jan 2017)](https://www.algonquincollege.com/languages/files/2019/08/manuel_candidat_e-tef_canada.pdf)
- Other rules candidates are given:
  - No dictionaries or reference material: "Les dictionnaires (papier ou électroniques)… ne sont pas autorisés". — [Livret du responsable 2026](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2026-livret-du-responsable-tef.pdf)
  - Scrap paper is allowed for the writing test only, on request, and is collected and destroyed afterwards. "la production écrite doit se faire sur le sujet d'expression écrite affichée à l'écran". — [Livret du surveillant 2025](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-livret-du-surveillanttef.pdf)
  - The 2017 handbook says drafts are not marked: "les feuilles de brouillon ne sont pas prises en compte". — [Manuel du candidat (2017)](https://www.algonquincollege.com/languages/files/2019/08/manuel_candidat_e-tef_canada.pdf)
- Computer delivery: "L'épreuve d'expression écrite se passe uniquement sur Paragraphe." Paper is only for proven *illectronisme* (no digital literacy), with FDA's approval. — [Livret du responsable 2026](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2026-livret-du-responsable-tef.pdf)
  - Indirect evidence about spellcheck: candidates with a motor disability may use their own tools "à condition que la correction orthographique automatique soit inactive". This implies the exam writing environment has no spellcheck or autocorrect. — [same](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2026-livret-du-responsable-tef.pdf)
- Comparison, not TEF Canada: TEF IRN (France residency/nationality) is 30 min, with A = 40 words / 10 min (an informal message asking for news) and B = 80 words / 20 min (a motivation text, formal). Prep content for IRN must not be mixed up with TEF Canada. — [Livret du surveillant 2025](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-livret-du-surveillanttef.pdf)
- PREP SITE: the live e-TEF word counter splits on spaces, hyphens and apostrophes ("l'entreprise" = 2, "c'est-à-dire" = 4). Suggested targets are 90–110 words (A) and 220–250 (B). Section A should be objective, third person, with no letter greetings. In B a letter layout is "optional". It cites no official document. — [open-exam-prep.com](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/tef-canada/written-expression/writing-overview)

### Inferences
- An AI reviewer should treat Section A as **news-report prose continuing the given lead**: same tense frame (passé composé/imparfait narrative), same characters and place, journalistic third person, several paragraphs, no opinion essay. It should check that the continuation is consistent with the given start and isn't a retelling of it.
- Section B should be checked as **a formal letter addressed to a newspaper**, taking a clear position on the quoted statement, with **≥3 distinct, developed arguments**. The official consigne says "lettre au journal", so letter conventions (salutation such as "Madame, Monsieur,", closing formula) are the safer default, even though one prep site calls them optional.
- Minimums are minimums. Aiming a little above (about 100 / about 230+ words) leaves margin for however the counter splits words.

### Gaps
- No official statement on how Paragraphe counts words, whether it shows a live counter, or whether a word maximum exists. Official documents give only minimums.
- No official text on the exact format of the screen (whether the given article start is repeated above the answer box, whether the candidate types a title).
- No official, more detailed candidate handbook (manuel du candidat) newer than January 2017 turned up. The 2017 one describes e-TEF and paper versions. Current delivery is described only in the 2025/2026 invigilator and centre-manager booklets.

## 2. Official evaluation criteria and grid

### Takeaway
OFFICIAL: two independent graders, specialists in language teaching, use a **standardised grid** with two families of criteria. (1) **Quality and effectiveness of the text**: fit to the situation, overall organisation, clarity of information or argument, ability to develop, nuance and make precise. (2) **Range and control of written French**: vocabulary, syntax (sentences, tenses, moods), spelling. The grid gives a **global CEFR performance level**. FDA does **not** publish the grid itself, the weights or per-criterion points.

### Cited Findings
- Verbatim from the official sample sheet: "L'épreuve d'expression écrite du TEF est évaluée par deux correcteurs, de façon indépendante, qui sont des spécialistes de la didactique des langues. Ils évaluent de façon objective :
  ➔ la qualité et l'efficacité des textes produits : • le respect de la situation • l'organisation générale du texte • la clarté de l'information ou de l'argumentation • la capacité à développer, nuancer, préciser ses propos
  ➔ la richesse et la maîtrise de la langue française écrite : • le lexique • la syntaxe (phrases, temps et modes…) • l'orthographe".
  The guiding questions printed beside them: "Le texte est-il clair, limpide, confus, incohérent ?"; "Les informations sont-elles sommaires, vagues, précises, détaillées ?"; "Les phrases sont-elles simples, complexes, maîtrisées ?"; "Les temps et modes sont-ils correctement utilisés ?"; "L'orthographe usuelle est-elle maîtrisée ?"; "Le lexique employé est-il juste, précis, adéquat ?" — [FDA sample sheet](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf)
- "Les correcteurs utilisent une grille d'évaluation standardisée. Celle-ci permet d'attribuer, grâce aux différents critères d'évaluation, un niveau de performance globale sur l'échelle du Cadre européen commun de référence pour les langues (CECR). A1 A2 B1 B2 C1 C2". — [same](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf)
  - The 2017 handbook says "au moins deux correcteurs" and that the grid "est la même pour tous les candidats. Elle est ensuite analysée par le Centre de langue française." — [Manuel du candidat (2017)](https://www.algonquincollege.com/languages/files/2019/08/manuel_candidat_e-tef_canada.pdf)
- Double marking is centralised at FDA, not done at the test centre: "Les épreuves d'expression écrite et d'expression orale du TEF font systématiquement l'objet d'une double correction/évaluation par la CCI Paris Île-de-France." Scripts go up automatically through Paragraphe "mises à disposition des évaluateurs du Français des affaires pour correction". — [Livret du surveillant 2025](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-livret-du-surveillanttef.pdf)
- Official CEFR descriptors for written expression (FDA's level interpretation grid). Verbatim:
  - A1: "Vous pouvez écrire de petits textes très simples."
  - A2: "Vous pouvez écrire des messages courts et simples."
  - B1: "Vous pouvez écrire un texte simple et cohérent sur des sujets familiers. Vous pouvez décrire vos expériences et vos impressions dans une lettre."
  - B2: "Vous pouvez écrire des textes clairs et détaillés sur différents sujets liés à vos intérêts. Vous pouvez transmettre une information et exposer votre opinion par écrit."
  - C1: "Vous pouvez écrire des textes structurés et développer votre point de vue sur des sujets complexes dans une lettre, un essai, un rapport. Vous pouvez vous adapter au style au destinataire."
  - C2: "Vous pouvez écrire un texte clair, fluide avec un style adapté. Vous avez un style efficace, tout à fait adapté au destinataire et à la situation."
  — [FDA, *Grille d'interprétation des niveaux TEF–CECR*](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/grille-interpretation-des-niveaux-tef-cecr.pdf)
- The results certificate gives a performance description for writing. Example at B2: "Produit un texte clair sur des sujets d'intérêt général, où les faits et les idées s'enchaînent de façon logique." — [FDA sample attestation TEF Canada (session 2024-10-06)](https://lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/exemple-attestation-tefcanada.pdf)
- PREP SITE: four "core dimensions" (task fulfilment / adéquation au sujet; coherence and cohesion; vocabulary range and accuracy; morphosyntax and spelling), presented as equally weighted, combined into one score out of 450 for both tasks. Not sourced. — [open-exam-prep.com](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/tef-canada/written-expression/writing-overview)
- Not TEF, flagged so it isn't confused: Université Laval's TEÉF publishes point weights per criterion (vocabulaire /15, ponctuation /5, etc.). That is a different test. — [ULaval TEÉF](https://www.flsh.ulaval.ca/ecole-de-langues/tests-de-competences-langagieres/test-dexpression-ecrite-en-francais-teef)

### Inferences
- For an AI reviewer the official criteria map onto seven checks.
  - Task and situation: respect de la situation (genre, addressee, register, continuing the given article, a letter to the paper).
  - Organisation: paragraphs, logical progression, connectors.
  - Clarity of information or argument.
  - Development and nuance: precise detail rather than vague, nuanced arguments, examples.
  - Lexique: accuracy, precision, range.
  - Syntaxe: sentence complexity and control, tense and mood use, including subjonctif after opinion and wish verbs, and narrative tense consistency in A.
  - Orthographe: spelling, including agreement.
- The grid gives a **global** CEFR level, not points per error, so feedback should weigh communicative effectiveness alongside error counts. The B2→C1 jump in the official descriptors is about structure, developing a point of view and **adapting style to the addressee**. C2 adds fluency and a style "tout à fait adapté au destinataire et à la situation".
- The "Description de la performance" (B2: facts and ideas linked logically) suggests cohesion and logical linking matter a lot.

### Gaps
- The actual grid (descriptor bands per criterion, weights, how two graders' disagreements are resolved, whether Sections A and B are weighted equally) isn't public. I found no official or leaked version, and FDA states no weights.
- No official sample scripts with grades were found for TEF Canada.

## 3. How scores map to TEF scales, NCLC/CLB and CEFR

### Takeaway
OFFICIAL: since **11 December 2023** the certificate reports each skill **out of 699**, plus an "Équivalence ancien score", which is **0–450 for Expression écrite**, a CEFR level and an NCLC level. For writing on the /699 scale, **NCLC 7 = 428–471**. On the old /450 scale, **NCLC 7 = 310–348**. As of the IRCC page modified **2026-10-16**, **Express Entry assesses TEF Canada on the "Équivalence ancien score" (/450) column only**. The /699 numbers must not be entered.

### Cited Findings
- FDA's official table, "Attribution des niveaux NCLC selon le score obtenu aux différentes épreuves du TEF Canada (score / 699)".
  - Old /699 table (**1 Oct 2019 – 10 Dec 2023**): the same bands for every skill. <NCLC 4 = 0–299; NCLC 4 = 300–349; 5 = 350–399; 6 = 400–449; 7 = 450–499; 8 = 500–532; 9 = 533–565; 10 = 566–699.
  - New table (**from 11 Dec 2023**) for **Expression écrite**: <NCLC 4 = 0–267; NCLC 4 = 268–329; 5 = 330–378; 6 = 379–427; **7 = 428–471**; 8 = 472–511; 9 = 512–557; 10 = 558–576; 11 = 577–618; 12 = 619–699.
  - New table, Compréhension écrite / orale (one shared column): 4 = 306–351; 5 = 352–392; 6 = 393–433; 7 = 434–461; 8 = 462–502; 9 = 503–545; 10 = 546–572; 11 = 573–614; 12 = 615–699.
  - New table, Expression orale: 4 = 328–386; 5 = 387–421; 6 = 422–455; 7 = 456–493; 8 = 494–517; 9 = 518–555; 10 = 556–591; 11 = 592–641; 12 = 642–699.
  — [FDA, *Correspondance Score TEF – Niveau NCLC* (uploaded 2024-10)](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/correspondance-tef-nclc.pdf)
  - Extraction caveat: the PDF's column layout was reconstructed from extracted text. The CE/CO shared column and the EE and EO columns are confirmed by IRCC's figures for CLB 6 after 10 Dec 2023: reading 393–433, writing 379–427, listening 393–433, speaking 422–455. — [IRCC, Rural Community Immigration Pilot language page (modified 2025-08-13)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/rural-franco-pilots/rural-immigration/eligibility/language-test.html)
- IRCC Express Entry (page modified **2026-10-16**): "Your language level will be based on the 'Équivalence ancien score' (previous score equivalency)." The page tells applicants to enter only that column's scores and warns "If you don't enter the correct scores, we may refuse your application". The /699 scores "aren't compatible with our system at this time." It also refers to tests "between December 11, 2023 and May 6, 2024" and says "This change has been reverted back to the Express Entry scoring grid we currently use." — [IRCC Express Entry language testing](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-requirements/language-testing.html)
  - IRCC writing bands on the /450 "ancien score": **CLB 10 = 393–450; 9 = 371–392; 8 = 349–370; 7 = 310–348; 6 = 271–309; 5 = 226–270; 4 = 181–225**. The other skills on that grid: reading /300 (CLB 7 = 207–232), listening /360 (CLB 7 = 249–279), speaking /450 (same bands as writing). — [same](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-requirements/language-testing.html)
  - The rural pilot page also keeps three TEF tables by test date: after 10 Dec 2023, 1 Oct 2019 – 10 Dec 2023, before 30 Sep 2019. — [IRCC rural pilot page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/rural-franco-pilots/rural-immigration/eligibility/language-test.html)
- Worked official example (certificate from the 6 Oct 2024 session): "Expression écrite 415 [/699] | 299 / 450 [ancien score] | B2 | NCLC6". 415 falls in the new EE band NCLC 6 (379–427). 299/450 falls in IRCC's CLB 6 (271–309). Both scales agree here. The certificate also shows CE 459 ↔ 230/300, CO 484 ↔ 289/360, EO 490 ↔ 344/450. — [FDA sample attestation](https://lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/exemple-attestation-tefcanada.pdf)
- The CEFR level is printed per skill on the certificate, but **no official public table converting /699 or /450 writing scores to CEFR** was found. The sample shows only that 415/699 = B2. — [same](https://lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/exemple-attestation-tefcanada.pdf)
- Programme thresholds on FDA's page: Express Entry NCLC 7 in all skills; Mobilité francophone NCLC 5; citizenship NCLC 4 (listening and speaking only). — [FDA TEF Canada page](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/tests-diplomes/test-evaluation-francais-tef/tef-canada/)
- Validity: two years from the certificate's issue date. "La période de validité des attestations de résultats de tous les TEF est de deux ans." — [Livret du responsable 2026](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2026-livret-du-responsable-tef.pdf)

### Inferences
- The usual writing target for Express Entry is **NCLC 7 = 310+/450 (ancien score)**, roughly **428+/699**. CLB 9 (371+/450), the level that earns the most Express Entry points, is about 512+/699. These are the numbers to show learners, with the IRCC column clearly labelled.
- The grid gives a CEFR level, and the official CEFR descriptors suggest NCLC 7 sits around solid B2. This is inferred, since no official CEFR↔/699 table was found. An AI's score *estimate* should be given as a CEFR/NCLC band, not a precise number.

### Gaps
- No official CEFR cut scores for EE on /699 or /450.
- No documentation of how the two sections' grid results combine into the /699 or /450 score.
- The IRCC FR equivalency-chart URL returned 404, and the EN chart page couldn't be retrieved (HTTP/2 errors). The figures above come from the Express Entry and rural pilot pages instead.

## 4. What is penalised (word count, off-topic, register, arguments, copying, incomplete)

### Takeaway
Official TEF documents set minimums (80 / 200 words, ≥3 arguments in B, several paragraphs in A) and grade "le respect de la situation", but **publish no defined penalty** for a word-count shortfall, off-topic text, copying the prompt or an unfinished text. Specific penalty rules found online come from prep sites or from **TCF Canada** (France Éducation international's "A1 non atteint" rule). They must not be presented as TEF rules.

### Cited Findings
- OFFICIAL requirements (failing them presumably costs marks under "respect de la situation", "organisation" and "capacité à développer"): "80 mots minimum… en faisant plusieurs paragraphes" (A); "200 mots minimum… Développez au moins 3 arguments" (B). — [FDA sample sheet](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf)
- OFFICIAL: both sections must be done ("Les deux sections, A et B, sont à traiter"). Only text in the on-screen answer counts; scrap paper is not marked. — [Manuel du candidat (2017)](https://www.algonquincollege.com/languages/files/2019/08/manuel_candidat_e-tef_canada.pdf); [Livret du surveillant 2025](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-livret-du-surveillanttef.pdf)
- OFFICIAL, administrative only: fraud or copying from another candidate means no certificate and a ban from the TEF. Abandoning a test or missing parts for non-medical reasons means no results ("ne reçoivent pas de résultats"; "pas l'attestation de résultats en cas d'abandon d'une ou plusieurs épreuves"). — [Livret du responsable 2026](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2026-livret-du-responsable-tef.pdf)
- PREP SITE, unverified: under 80 words (e.g. 75) "triggers an automatic word-penalty reduction across all four criteria"; 185 words in B "will cap the maximum attainable score" below NCLC 7. No source given. — [open-exam-prep.com](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/tef-canada/written-expression/writing-overview)
- PREP SITE claim tracing back to TCF Canada, not TEF: a writing script may be graded « A1 non atteint » if each task doesn't reach the required word count or is off-topic. This was reported in search results as from France Éducation international's TCF Canada page. That page blocked access (ERR-BOT-403), so it wasn't seen directly. — [France Éducation international, TCF Canada](https://france-education-international.fr/test/tcf-canada) (via search summary only)

### Inferences
- An AI reviewer should **flag** being under minimum, too few arguments, a single-paragraph Section A, a Section A not written as a news report or opinionated instead, a Section B not written as a letter, missing a clear stance, or reusing the prompt's sentences. These are risks under the official "respect de la situation" and development criteria. It should describe them as **likely to lower the level**, not as a fixed deduction, since TEF publishes none.
- Copying the quoted statement or the article lead adds words that probably show no language ability. Advice to paraphrase is reasonable but has no official backing.

### Gaps
- No official TEF rule found on word-count shortfall, off-topic text, wrong genre, copied prompt text or unfinished text. That should be confirmed with FDA (tef@cci-paris-idf.fr) before any hard rule is put in the product.

## 5. Changes to the format, 2023–2026

### Takeaway
The **writing tasks have stayed the same** (A: fait divers, 80 words, 25 min; B: letter, 200 words, 35 min) from at least the 2017 handbook to the 2026 booklets. The real changes were: (1) the **new /699 score scale and NCLC tables from 11 December 2023**, which IRCC briefly applied and then reverted, so it now uses the /450 "ancien score" for Express Entry; (2) the reading and listening tests were reformatted in December 2023, writing and speaking reportedly unchanged; (3) delivery now runs **only on computer in the Paragraphe app**, results are digital-only, and paper is an exception.

### Cited Findings
- Same writing format in the January 2017 candidate handbook (an e-TEF and paper era: "certains centres organisent uniquement la version papier-crayon") as in the 2024/2025 sample sheet and the 2025 invigilator booklet. — [Manuel du candidat e-TEF Canada, janvier 2017](https://www.algonquincollege.com/languages/files/2019/08/manuel_candidat_e-tef_canada.pdf); [FDA sample sheet](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/tef-exemples-epreuve-ee.pdf); [Livret du surveillant 2025](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-livret-du-surveillanttef.pdf)
- New /699 tables "à partir du 11 décembre 2023". — [FDA correspondance TEF–NCLC](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/correspondance-tef-nclc.pdf). ### Inferences
- Writing practice in Ink can safely mirror the 2026 format: two separately timed tasks, typed, no spellcheck or dictionary. A "mock" mode should drop autocorrect and lookups to match the exam.
- Score talk in the product should show the /450 "ancien score" for IRCC purposes, with /699 shown as what's printed on the certificate. The two are easy to confuse.

### Gaps
- FDA's own 2023-changes page couldn't be read, so "writing unchanged in 2023" rests on the format matching across official documents plus prep-site summaries.
- No official 2026 announcement of further changes was found. Also unknown: whether IRCC will adopt the /699 scale later, and whether a remote TEF Canada exists.
