# Ink design system

Gray-first and quiet, after Linear: a near-black floor in dark mode, surfaces that lift in small steps, hairline edges, pill-shaped actions. Color carries meaning only (mistake categories, ok/warn), and brand blue appears at most once per page. The essay is the loudest thing on screen; chrome recedes.

## Rules that apply everywhere

1. **Use a component from `components/ui/` before writing markup.** Its file is the source of truth: the `cva` variant and size unions list everything that exists. An unlisted variant or size is a bug, not an option. TypeScript rejects invented values; do not cast around it.
2. **Never style a component's look through `className`.** `className` is for layout only: margin, width, `flex`/`grid` placement, `self-*`, `order`. Never color, background, border, radius, font size, height or padding. A look that no variant gives you is a design decision; ask, don't improvise.
3. **Tokens only, semantic only.** Colors come from [color.md](docs/design/color.md), sizes from [type.md](docs/design/type.md), shadows and radii from [surfaces.md](docs/design/surfaces.md). Tailwind's own palette, font sizes, shadows and radii are deleted in `app/globals.css`, so `bg-zinc-100`, `text-sm`, `shadow-md` or `rounded-lg` compile to **nothing, silently**. No hex, `rgb()`, `oklch()` or arbitrary values (`bg-[#…]`, `text-[13px]`) in components.
4. **One primary action per view.** Two dark filled buttons means the screen has no next step. See the flowchart in [buttons.md](docs/design/buttons.md).
5. **Brand blue at most once per page**, never in the writing or correction screens: blue there competes with the essay and the category colors.
6. **Every control is labelled.** Icon-only buttons get `aria-label` and a Tooltip; inputs get a `<label>` or `aria-label`. A placeholder is never the label.
7. **Shortened text ends on a whole word.** Any one-line text that can run out of room (titles, prompts, rules, list rows) uses `<TruncateWords text={…} />` from `components/truncate-words.tsx`, never Tailwind's `truncate` or `text-ellipsis`, which cut mid-word ("devrait-il dev…"). It takes all the room its row gives it (`flex-1`), keeps the full text for screen readers and on hover, and falls back to a character cut only for a single word too long to fit.
8. **Copy: less is more.** Every string on screen, and everything the AI writes into it, uses the fewest words that still say what to do or what happened. Cut what the screen already shows (counts in the app bar, the text in a field, a button's own label), reasons nobody asked for, and sentence-ending periods on fragments ("No essays yet", not "No essays yet."). The AI's limits live in `lib/ai/correction.ts` (rule under 40 characters, one-sentence explanation, a few words of hint).

## Which component?

```
Does the user trigger an action?
 ├── Yes, and it navigates to another page → Next <Link> styled with buttonVariants() — buttons.md
 ├── Yes → <Button> — buttons.md
 └── No
      ├── Filtering, several values on at once (mistake categories) → ToggleGroup multiple — toggles-and-tabs.md
      ├── Exactly one view or filter at a time (Section A/B, All / Mock / Practice) → Tabs — toggles-and-tabs.md
      ├── Typing text → Input (one line) or Textarea (several) — forms.md
      ├── Extra info about something on screen → Tooltip (hover hint) or Popover (click) — overlays.md
      └── Reporting what happened → toast (Sonner) — feedback.md
```

## Product components

Built, in `app/home/` and `components/`:
- **`AppSidebar`** (`components/app-sidebar.tsx`, on shadcn's `Sidebar`, `collapsible="offcanvas"`): the logo, New essay (`outline`, because the page owns the primary action), then every essay as one flat list, most recently edited first. Don't add sections, groups, a user footer or nav items without a decision; it was cut down on purpose. On phones it's a sheet behind `SidebarTrigger`.
- **`ContributionGraph`** (`app/home/contribution-graph.tsx`): the year of essays, 53 weeks ending today, cells sized to the column (`rounded-cell`, five `bg-text/*` levels), hover detail in the caption. Data is one `{ date, words }` per essay, in the user's local days.
- **Essays** (`lib/essays.ts`): `useEssays()`, `useStartEssay()` (opens `/write?essay=<id>&new=1` on a blank page and saves nothing), `saveEssay()` / `removeEssay()`. An essay is saved only once its title or text has something in it (`hasWriting`), and removed again if cleared back to nothing; its draft goes too when the page closes. Starting an essay never asks anything first: no form, dialog or popover.

- **`AppBar`** (`components/app-bar.tsx`): the sticky bar on the writing and correction screens. It spans the full window, never the essay's column, so the crumb on the left and the run details, clock, word count and correction progress on the right have room. Put it outside `<main>`; `AppBarSkeleton` holds its place while a screen loads. Home keeps its own bar inside `SidebarInset`.

- **`TruncateWords`** (`components/truncate-words.tsx`): one line that ends on a whole word and "…" when it's too long (rule 7). In a flex row, give its row `min-w-0` and let it be the part that shrinks; put fixed parts (a "Home /" crumb, a date) beside it with `shrink-0`.

Built, in `app/write/`:
- **`EssayScreen`** (`essay-screen.tsx`): a user's essay at `/write?essay=<id>`: an "Untitled" title field (their prompt or any name; Enter goes to the body) and the `EssayEditor`. No exam framing. The sample exam runs (`/write?section=…&run=…`) still use `WritingScreen` with the timer.
- **`EssayEditor`** (`essay-editor.tsx`): any surface where the user writes essay text. Never a `Textarea` and never a third-party editor for essay text. `value`/`defaultValue`/`onValueChange` take `Block[]` from `blocks.ts`; `placeholder` in French. Spellcheck is on, in the editor's `lang` (French), except in mock runs (`spellCheck={false}`: the exam has none); autocorrect, Grammarly and LanguageTool stay off. Enter in an empty paragraph adds another empty one. Selection crosses paragraphs: ⌘A/Ctrl+A selects the whole essay, and Shift+arrows past an edge, Shift+click and dragging out of a paragraph extend into the next; copy puts a blank line between paragraphs, and typing, deleting or pasting replaces the whole selection. Its caret is its focus indicator; don't add an outline around paragraphs. Paragraphs reorder three ways: drag the grip handle, tap or click it for Move up / Move down (the non-drag alternative WCAG 2.5.7 requires), or ⌘⇧↑/↓ (Ctrl+Shift on Windows); each move is one undo step and is announced to screen readers. With a mouse, handles show on hovering a paragraph, in the left margin at `sm` and up, which needs 40px of margin: put the editor in a container with `sm:px-10` (as `writing-screen.tsx` does). On touch screens only the focused paragraph has a handle (hidden ones can't catch a tap): in the left margin on tablets, in the gap above the paragraph at the right on phones. `readOnly` hides them. The essay is one Tab stop (the last paragraph that had focus); arrows move between paragraphs, and ↑ on the first line or ← at the very start leaves through `onExitTop` (the essay screen sends it to the title).
- **`WordCount`** (`writing-screen.tsx`): `142 / 200 words` below the minimum, `✓ 214 words` at or above it. Count words with `countWords()` only, so every screen agrees.
- **`ExamClock`** (`writing-screen.tsx`, logic in `timer.ts`): the run's countdown, 25 min for Section A and 35 for B (`EXAM_MINUTES`). Always computed from stored timestamps, never counted. Practice: starts on the first keystroke, can pause (the essay locks while paused), runs into overtime (`+1:05`). Mock: starts from an explicit "Start mock run", never pauses, keeps running if the page is closed, stops at `0:00` and locks the essay. The clock turns `text-warn` at 5:00 left; screen readers hear 5 min, 1 min and time's up, never every second.

- **`<Correction>`** (`correction.tsx`): Margin, the correction view. **The text never moves for a correction**: the review uses the editor's column and spacing, with only the header row added above the essay (`ReviewView`'s `heading` is whatever the screen shows above its editor), and empty paragraphs keep their line. `Correction.Header` holds the status, above the essay, and `Correction.Aside`, which heads the notes column level with the status (the filters, or the review trace while a review runs) and drops under the status when notes stack; the first paragraph's notes start below it. `Correction.Layout` places each paragraph's `Correction.Notes` in the space right of the essay, beside their paragraph; a tall open note pushes only the notes below it, by transform. It needs about 1184px of window for a 200px column; below that it falls back to notes under each paragraph. Marks keep text metrics: the number is a superscript that takes no width (`text-sup`, raised just past the phrase's last letter, in the category color), padding cancelled by negative margin, a 1.5px underline, and the fix input the mark's exact box (one line box, `h-[1lh]`, `align-top`, same padding and underline), so opening a mark moves no letters. Neither has a focus ring, which would paint over the neighboring words: focus is the full-strength tint and underline against quiet resting marks (color.md). Keep those when touching marks. `Correction.Filters` (category ToggleGroup, only categories present, count left per chip; nothing pressed shows all; `sm` chips in the notes column, `lg` when they sit above the essay, where touch screens see them), `Correction.Row`, `Correction.Paragraph`, `Correction.Notes` (one note per mark: rule, explanation, memory line, feedback, Check / Hint or Show answer / Skip). Read-only text, never contenteditable. Category color comes from the `TONE` map in that file, as tints at the strengths color.md allows; add a category there, never inline. The root takes `mistakes` (already placed in the current text), `onCheck`, `onFix`, `onChange`, and controlled or uncontrolled `activeId` and `filter`. Each mistake is one Tab stop, its mark (note headers are clickable but out of the Tab order), and tabbing onto a mark opens it like a click (keyboard focus only). Enter on the input checks; Tab goes straight to that note's Check / Hint / Skip and Shift+Tab back; Tab past the last button opens the next mistake in reading order, even one later in the same paragraph; Escape, from the input or the note, closes and returns focus to the mark; a correct fix moves to the next open mark. Marks' hit area is the full line height (taller would overlap the next line's marks); on phones the note under the paragraph is the 44px target. Opening a note grows the card from its header and closing shrinks it back (motion.md); the notes below follow it in step. Wrong fixes shake the input (WAAPI, skipped under reduced motion). Notes text is English with French in `*asterisks*`, set in Literata italic.
- **`<Dictionary>`** (`dictionary.tsx`): the word lookup, decided in AGENTS.md (Dictionary). Wrap the screen in `<Dictionary enabled={…}>` (off in mock runs and in review), put `<Dictionary.Trigger />` first in the `AppBar` and `<Dictionary.Margin />` first in the essay's `<main>`, which must be `relative`. Wide windows (about 1312px) get the panel in the left margin, sticky as the essay scrolls, with no border or panel of its own; the essay never moves for it. Hidden, it folds to a ghost "Dictionary ⌘K" button in the same spot. Narrower, the Trigger (book icon) opens the same lookup in a Sheet from the left. Inside: the language Pair (`[your language ▾] ⇄ Français`, the side you type in on the left), an Input, then the result or the recent words. ⌘K/Ctrl+K opens it or focuses the field; Escape returns to the last essay paragraph. Lookups wait 300ms after typing stops. Settings (language, direction, open, recent) are in localStorage under `ink:dictionary:settings`.
- **Review** (`review.tsx`): `useReviewFlow` (open or reuse a review, run the check), `ReviewView` (loading, failed, empty and all-resolved states around `<Correction>`; while loading, `ReviewTrace` shows what the check is really doing, "Reading 4 paragraphs" then "Found 3 mistakes" counting up, in the filter chips' place, and their height above the essay, so the essay doesn't shift; never steps on a timer), `ReviewProgress` ("4 of 13 resolved" and a Progress bar for the app bar). The Review / Edit button sits at the right of the `AppBar`, `outline` `sm`.

Not built yet; build them natively (never from shadcn) when a screen needs them: lookup panel, the phone Drawer for Margin's notes (they stack under each paragraph for now), essay history row, category trend bars. Build it as a named component (`function EssayHistoryRow…`) inside the screen file that first needs it, from `components/ui/` parts and tokens; move it to `components/` once a second or third screen copies it.
