# Ink design system

Gray-first and quiet, after Linear: a near-black floor in dark mode, surfaces that lift in small steps, hairline edges, pill-shaped actions. Color carries meaning only (mistake categories, ok/warn), and brand blue appears at most once per page. The essay is the loudest thing on screen; chrome recedes.

## Rules that apply everywhere

1. **Use a component from `components/ui/` before writing markup.** Its file is the source of truth: the `cva` variant and size unions list everything that exists. An unlisted variant or size is a bug, not an option. TypeScript rejects invented values; do not cast around it.
2. **Never style a component's look through `className`.** `className` is for layout only: margin, width, `flex`/`grid` placement, `self-*`, `order`. Never color, background, border, radius, font size, height or padding. A look that no variant gives you is a design decision; ask, don't improvise.
3. **Tokens only, semantic only.** Colors come from [color.md](docs/design/color.md), sizes from [type.md](docs/design/type.md), shadows and radii from [surfaces.md](docs/design/surfaces.md). Tailwind's own palette, font sizes, shadows and radii are deleted in `app/globals.css`, so `bg-zinc-100`, `text-sm`, `shadow-md` or `rounded-lg` compile to **nothing, silently**. No hex, `rgb()`, `oklch()` or arbitrary values (`bg-[#…]`, `text-[13px]`) in components.
4. **One primary action per view.** Two dark filled buttons means the screen has no next step. See the flowchart in [buttons.md](docs/design/buttons.md).
5. **Brand blue at most once per page**, never in the writing or correction screens: blue there competes with the essay and the category colors.
6. **Every control is labelled.** Icon-only buttons get `aria-label` and a Tooltip; inputs get a `<label>` or `aria-label`. A placeholder is never the label.
7. **Shortened text ends on a whole word.** Any one-line text that can run out of room (titles, prompts, rules, list rows) uses `<TruncateWords text={…} />` from `components/truncate-words.tsx`, never Tailwind's `truncate` or `text-ellipsis`, which cut mid-word ("devrait-il dev…"). It takes all the room its row gives it (`flex-1`), keeps the full text for screen readers and on hover, and falls back to a character cut only for a single word too long to fit.
8. **The essay's column holds only the writer's words.** Interface copy (status, instructions, summaries, hints) goes beside the text, in the notes column on the right or the dictionary's margin on the left, never between the title and the essay or between paragraphs. Only where there's no room beside it (phones, narrow windows) does it stack above the text.
9. **Copy: less is more.** Every string on screen, and everything the AI writes into it, uses the fewest words that still say what to do or what happened. Cut what the screen already shows (counts in the app bar, the text in a field, a button's own label), reasons nobody asked for, and sentence-ending periods on fragments ("No essays yet", not "No essays yet."). The AI's limits live in `lib/ai/correction.ts` (rule under 40 characters, one-sentence explanation, a few words of hint).

## Which component?

```
Does the user trigger an action?
 ├── Yes, and it navigates to another page → Next <Link> styled with buttonVariants() — buttons.md
 ├── Yes → <Button> — buttons.md
 └── No
      ├── Filtering, several values on at once (mistake categories) → ToggleGroup multiple — toggles-and-tabs.md
      ├── Exactly one view or filter at a time (Section A/B, All / Mock / Practice) → Tabs — toggles-and-tabs.md
      ├── Typing text → Input (one line) or Textarea (several) — forms.md
      ├── Extra info about something on screen → Tooltip (hover hint) or Popover (click) — overlays.md
      └── Reporting what happened → toast (Sonner) — feedback.md
```

## Product components

Built, in `app/home/` and `components/`:
- **`AppSidebar`** (`components/app-sidebar.tsx`, on shadcn's `Sidebar`, `collapsible="offcanvas"`): the logo, New essay (`outline`, because the page owns the primary action), then every essay as one flat list, most recently edited first. Don't add sections, groups, a user footer or nav items without a decision; it was cut down on purpose. On phones it's a sheet behind `SidebarTrigger`.
- **`ThemeMenu`** (`app/home/home-screen.tsx`): Light / Dark / System, a ghost icon button at the right of Home's bar. Only there: the theme is set rarely, and the writing screens' bars stay quiet. `setTheme()` and `useTheme()` from `lib/theme.ts`; never read `prefers-color-scheme` for colors directly.
- **`ContributionGraph`** (`app/home/contribution-graph.tsx`): the year of essays, 53 weeks ending today, cells sized to the column (`rounded-cell`, five `bg-text/*` levels), hover detail in the caption. Data is one `{ date, words }` per essay, in the user's local days.
- **Essays** (`lib/essays.ts`): `useEssays()`, `useStartEssay()` (opens `/write?essay=<id>&new=1` on a blank page and saves nothing), `saveEssay()` / `removeEssay()`. An essay is saved only once its title or text has something in it (`hasWriting`), and removed again if cleared back to nothing; its draft goes too when the page closes. Starting an essay never asks anything first: no form, dialog or popover.

- **`AppBar`** (`components/app-bar.tsx`): the sticky bar on the writing and correction screens. It spans the full window, never the essay's column, so the crumb on the left and the run details, clock, word count and correction progress on the right have room. Put it outside `<main>`; `AppBarSkeleton` holds its place while a screen loads. Home keeps its own bar inside `SidebarInset`.

- **`TruncateWords`** (`components/truncate-words.tsx`): one line that ends on a whole word and "…" when it's too long (rule 7). Essay titles pass `keepLastWord`, which keeps the title's last word after the ellipsis ("Toutes les étudiants… diplôme"; a spaced "?" stays with its word) for as long as one word still fits before it: the breadcrumb, the sidebar and Home's Recent all do. In a flex row, give its row `min-w-0` and let it be the part that shrinks; put fixed parts (a "Home /" crumb, a date) beside it with `shrink-0`.

Built, in `app/write/`:
- **`EssayScreen`** (`essay-screen.tsx`): a user's essay at `/write?essay=<id>`: an "Untitled" title field (their prompt or any name, at most `TITLE_MAX` characters from `lib/essays.ts`; Enter goes to the body) and the `EssayEditor`. Its app bar holds the dictionary trigger, the crumb, the coffee-mode toggle (a ghost `icon-sm` `Coffee` button with `aria-pressed`), `EssayTimer`, the review's progress and Review. Coffee mode fades out everything but the text, the word count and its own button (`invisible opacity-0`, so nothing moves), and leaves the dictionary in place without its recent words (`<Dictionary quiet>`). ⌘S / Ctrl+S saves the draft and the essay list at once instead of the browser's Save page, with a "Saved" toast ("Nothing to save" before anything's written). The word count is fixed at the bottom left (`text-label text-text-3`, on `bg-bg` since text scrolls under it); with a selection it reads "N of M words", from `EssayEditor`'s `onSelectedText`. One view: a review's marks and notes go on the editable essay.
- **`EssayEditor`** (`essay-editor.tsx`): any surface where the user writes essay text. Never a `Textarea` and never a third-party editor for essay text. `value`/`defaultValue`/`onValueChange` take `Block[]` from `blocks.ts`; `placeholder` in French. Spellcheck is on, in the editor's `lang` (French); autocorrect, Grammarly and LanguageTool stay off. Enter at the end of a line starts a new line in the same paragraph; Enter again on that empty line removes it and starts a new paragraph; Enter mid-line or in an empty paragraph splits the paragraph (`pressEnter` in `blocks.ts`). Selection crosses paragraphs: ⌘A/Ctrl+A selects the whole essay, and Shift+arrows past an edge, Shift+click and dragging out of a paragraph extend into the next; copy puts a blank line between paragraphs, and typing, deleting or pasting replaces the whole selection. Its caret is its focus indicator; don't add an outline around paragraphs. `autoFocus` puts the caret back where the writer left it: screens save what `onLeave` reports with the draft (`useCaret` in `draft-store.ts`) and pass it as `defaultCaret`, so returning from Home or a reload lands on the same spot, scrolled to the middle of the window if it's off screen. With no saved caret nothing takes focus: never a guess like the end of the essay. Clicking the gap between two paragraphs puts the caret in the nearer one at the clicked x; clicking any other empty space (below the essay, its padding) leaves focus where it was. Nothing ever sends the caret to the last paragraph on its own. Paragraphs reorder three ways: drag the grip handle, tap or click it for Move up / Move down (the non-drag alternative WCAG 2.5.7 requires), or ⌘⇧↑/↓ (Ctrl+Shift on Windows); each move is one undo step and is announced to screen readers. With a mouse, handles show on hovering a paragraph, in the left margin at `sm` and up, which needs 40px of margin: put the editor in a container with `sm:px-10` (as `essay-screen.tsx` does). On touch screens only the focused paragraph has a handle (hidden ones can't catch a tap): in the left margin on tablets, in the gap above the paragraph at the right on phones. `readOnly` hides them. `renderUnderlay(block)` draws under a paragraph's text in a layer with the same metrics (text transparent, hidden from assistive tech: the review's marks); `renderAfter(block)` adds to a paragraph's block (its notes); `onTextSelect` reports caret moves, `onTextKeyDown` sees keys first (preventDefault takes them), `onValueChange`'s second argument is where the caret landed, and `handle.select(blockId, start, end?)` puts the caret somewhere and scrolls it into view. The essay is one Tab stop (the last paragraph that had focus); arrows move between paragraphs, and ↑ on the first line or ← at the very start leaves through `onExitTop` (the essay screen sends it to the title).
- **Word count** (in `essay-screen.tsx`'s app bar): `214 words`. Count words with `countWords()` only, so every screen agrees.
- **Grammar underlines** (`grammar.tsx`, server side `lib/grammar.ts`): decided in AGENTS.md (Grammar underlines). `useGrammar(blocks, enabled)` checks paragraphs 600ms after they settle and returns each one's issues; `GrammarMarks` draws them in the editor's `renderUnderlay`, `absolute inset-0` over the review's `Correction.Marks` (same text, transparent): `underline decoration-wavy decoration-1 decoration-warn/70 underline-offset-[5px]`, one style for grammar and spelling. `GrammarTip` (one per screen, given the editor's id) shows the explanation in a non-modal `Popover` anchored to the underline (`side="bottom"`, `w-auto max-w-72 p-2.5 text-caption`, `lang="fr"`, `initialFocus`/`finalFocus` off so the caret stays in the essay) on mouse hover after 250ms, kept open while the pointer is on it and for 200ms after it leaves, or with the caret strictly inside the words. Under the explanation, at the bottom right, "Answer": a text button, no padding (`text-caption text-text-3`, `hover:text-text`, a `::before` for its hit area; its mousedown prevented, so the caret stays). It swaps for the corrections in `font-serif` `text-ui`, joined with " · ". Never a note in the margin, never the answer in the text. The essay's `EssayEditor` gets `spellCheck={false}`: Grammalecte does spelling.
- **`EssayTimer`** (`essay-timer.tsx`, logic in `timer.ts`): decided in AGENTS.md (Timer: Edge). Put it in the `AppBar` outside coffee mode's fade and pass `quiet={coffee}`: it fades its own buttons and keeps its line, which is positioned against the bar's sticky `<header>` (nothing positioned between them). Idle, a ghost `icon-sm` timer button opens a Popover (`w-60`) with the title, `DurationWheel` (`duration-wheel.tsx`) and an `xs` Start, "Count up" at all zero. The wheels: three columns (`h-40`, rows `h-8` `text-body` tabular figures, unit in `text-label` `text-text-3`) on one `bg-sunken` `rounded-row` band; rows fade and shrink away from it on a scroll-driven animation (`duration-wheel.css`; a mask fade where unsupported), so scrolling never re-renders a row. Mouse drags write `scrollTop` with snapping off and fling on release; touch scrolls natively. Each value crossing the band plays `wheel-tick.ts`'s Web Audio click. Running, a ghost `icon-xs` Pause/Resume beside the icon, the time on the icon's tooltip, and a 2px line over the bar's bottom border (`bg-text-3`, `bg-border-strong` paused, `bg-warn` in the last 5 minutes) driven by one Web Animation per start, pause or resume, never per tick. The popover then holds the time (`text-title`, `role="timer"`), the limit or state in `text-caption` `text-text-3`, and ghost `icon-xs` Pause/Resume and Stop. Always computed from timestamps, never counted. A countdown stops at 0:00; a count-up stops after 4 hours; both say so in a toast. Screen readers hear 5 min and 1 min left, never every second. State lives in the component: leaving the essay or closing the window ends it, and nothing is saved.

- **`<Correction>`** (`correction.tsx`): Margin, the correction view, on the editable essay. **The text never moves for a correction**: `Correction.Layout` wraps the screen's title and `EssayEditor`; the editor draws `Correction.Marks` in its underlay (`renderUnderlay`) and `Correction.Notes` in each paragraph's block (`renderAfter`), and spreads `useCorrectionText()` (caret → open note; in the open mark Enter checks a changed rewrite, Escape closes the note, Tab goes to its buttons). All the review's own copy ("Reviewing your essay…" and its trace, the summary when there's one, the filters) goes in `Correction.Aside`, inside `Correction.Header`: with room it heads the notes column, level with the title, and the header takes no room above the essay; when notes stack it sits above the essay. No instruction copy ("Click a highlight…", "Enter to check"): the highlights and notes speak for themselves. `Correction.Layout` places each paragraph's Notes in the space right of the essay, beside their paragraph; a tall open note pushes only the notes below it, by transform. It needs about 1184px of window for a 200px column; below that it falls back to notes under each paragraph. Marks keep text metrics: the number is a superscript that takes no width (`text-sup`, raised just past the phrase's last letter, in the category color), padding cancelled by negative margin, a 1.5px underline. The mark whose note is open is the full-strength tint and underline against quiet resting marks (color.md); resolved marks keep only an underline (`ok` solid when fixed, dotted when shown). Keep those when touching marks. `Correction.Filters` (category ToggleGroup, only categories present, count left per chip; nothing pressed shows all; `sm` chips in the notes column, `lg` when they sit above the essay, where touch screens see them), `Correction.Notes` (one note per mark: rule, explanation, memory line, feedback, hint, answer, then `xs` buttons: Check (primary), Hint and Show answer (`secondary`, each gone once used; Hint also goes after a wrong try, which shows it), and Skip (`ghost`, pushed to the end). Show answer works any time and shows the fix in the note, never in the text; a ghost `icon-xs` button with the return arrow (`CornerDownLeft`) beside it ("Use answer") puts it in. A fix typed or put in after the answer was shown counts as shown, not fixed). Never contenteditable. Category color comes from the `TONE` map in that file, as tints at the strengths color.md allows; add a category there, never inline. The root takes `mistakes` (already placed in the current text, with `current`, the words there now), `onCheck`, `onResolve`, `onChange`, `onFocusMark`, and controlled or uncontrolled `activeId` and `filter`. Note headers are clickable (opening one puts the caret at the end of its words) but out of the Tab order; the way in from the keyboard is the caret in a mark, then Tab. Shift+Tab from the first button goes back to the words; Tab past the last moves the caret to the next mistake in reading order; Escape in a note returns the caret to its words with the note closed; a correct fix moves the caret to the next open mark. Opening a note grows the card from its header and closing shrinks it back (motion.md); the notes below follow it in step. Wrong fixes shake the note (WAAPI, skipped under reduced motion). Notes text is English with French in `*asterisks*`, set in Literata italic.
- **`<Dictionary>`** (`dictionary.tsx`): the word lookup, decided in AGENTS.md (Dictionary). Wrap the screen in `<Dictionary>` (`enabled` turns it off), put `<Dictionary.Trigger />` first in the `AppBar` and `<Dictionary.Margin />` first in the essay's `<main>`, which must be `relative`. Wide windows (about 1312px) get the panel in the left margin, sticky as the essay scrolls, with no border or panel of its own; the essay never moves for it. Hidden, it folds to a ghost "Dictionary ⌘K" button in the same spot. Narrower, the Trigger (book icon) opens the same lookup in a Sheet from the left. Inside: the language Pair (`[your language ▾] ⇄ Français`, the side you type in on the left), an Input, then the result or the recent words, each with a ghost `icon-xs` X that removes it (shown on the row's hover or its own focus, always on touch screens). ⌘K/Ctrl+K opens it or focuses the field; Escape returns to the last essay paragraph. Lookups wait 300ms after typing stops. Settings (language, direction, open, recent) are in localStorage under `ink:dictionary:settings`. Selecting French in the essay or its title (1–8 words, one paragraph; `selection.ts` widens it to whole words) shows a "Translate ⌘K" pill above it; the pill or ⌘K grows it into a card with the French and its meaning in the writer's language. The meaning is fetched when the pill appears (one shared request per word, kept for the session), so the card fills at once. It never takes focus, so typing still replaces the selection; Escape or a click elsewhere closes it; on touch screens it sits below the selection. With words selected, ⌘K translates them instead of opening the panel. It comes with `<Dictionary>`; screens add nothing for it. Wherever a lookup shows French (the panel's result, the selection card), a ghost `icon-xs` speaker reads it aloud in French at 0.9 speed through `speakFrench()` from `lib/speech.ts` (the browser's own voice: Premium/Enhanced/Natural first, then known good ones, France's preferred, never macOS's character voices like Eddy or Grandma while anything else exists). Without a French voice on the device there's no button. When the French side is a noun, `lookUp` returns its gender (`Gender` from `lib/gender.ts`) and `NounGender` shows it under the translation in both places: French in `font-serif` `text-ui` `text-text-2`, with the indefinite article ("une maison", "un or une élève"; the singular for a plural), or a list with one line per gender and its English gloss in `text-text-3` ("un tour · a turn, a trip"). A noun only used in the plural gets "les vacances" and its gender named in `text-text-3` ("· feminine plural"), the one place a gender is spelled out, since no article shows it. It arrives with the translation, so there's no loading state of its own; no gender, no line. No abbreviations ("nf") and no hint copy around it. When the French side is a verb form, `lookUp` returns it too and `conjugation.tsx` shows it (decided in AGENTS.md, Dictionary: Conjugation): in the panel, `VerbEntry` under the translation and gender: the verb line (infinitive in `font-serif` `text-text-2`, then group and auxiliary in `text-text-3`, breaking only at the " · "), an `outline` `xs` tense menu (DropdownMenu, `w-56`, a radio group per mood), the rows, and "Also" links to the form's other readings. In the selection card, `SelectionVerb` under the meaning: the verb line with tense and person, then the rows, with a `line` Tabs per reading when there are several; the card is fixed at `w-74` for a verb so switching tabs never resizes it. Rows are `font-serif` `text-ui`, pronouns and agreement in `text-text-3`, the writer's person on `bg-sunken` (`rounded-row`, `aria-current`); each part of a form is `whitespace-nowrap` (the participle with its agreement as one) and a person's form wraps under itself with a hanging indent, never into the next row. Keep those when touching rows: they're what keeps the longest forms inside the 232px margin. A word that is both a noun and a verb shows both in the panel and one in the card (the server picks from the words before). The panel's scroller clips its x-axis, so buttons' invisible touch areas never make it scroll sideways.
- **Review** (`review.tsx`): `useReviewFlow` (run a review unless the text is unchanged since the last one, which is a toast), `EssayReview` (`<Correction>` and its Layout around the title and editor; fixes and Use answer go into the essay through it), `ReviewAside` (between the title and the editor: while checking, `ReviewTrace` shows what the check is really doing, "Reading 4 paragraphs" then "Found 3 mistakes" counting up, never steps on a timer; then the "No mistakes" / "All N done" summary and the filters), `ReviewProgress` ("4 of 13 done" and a Progress bar for the app bar). The Review button sits at the right of the `AppBar`, `outline` `sm`, "Reviewing…" and disabled while it runs.

Not built yet; build them natively (never from shadcn) when a screen needs them: lookup panel, the phone Drawer for Margin's notes (they stack under each paragraph for now), essay history row, category trend bars. Build it as a named component (`function EssayHistoryRow…`) inside the screen file that first needs it, from `components/ui/` parts and tokens; move it to `components/` once a second or third screen copies it.
