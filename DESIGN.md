# Ink design system

Gray-first and quiet, after Linear: a near-black floor in dark mode, surfaces that lift in small steps, hairline edges, pill-shaped actions. Color carries meaning only (mistake categories, ok/warn), and brand blue appears at most once per page. The essay is the loudest thing on screen; chrome recedes.

## Rules that apply everywhere

1. **Use a component from `components/ui/` before writing markup.** Its file is the source of truth: the `cva` variant and size unions list everything that exists. An unlisted variant or size is a bug, not an option. TypeScript rejects invented values; do not cast around it.
2. **Never style a component's look through `className`.** `className` is for layout only: margin, width, `flex`/`grid` placement, `self-*`, `order`. Never color, background, border, radius, font size, height or padding. A look that no variant gives you is a design decision; ask, don't improvise.
3. **Tokens only, semantic only.** Colors come from [color.md](docs/design/color.md), sizes from [type.md](docs/design/type.md), shadows and radii from [surfaces.md](docs/design/surfaces.md). Tailwind's own palette, font sizes, shadows and radii are deleted in `app/globals.css`, so `bg-zinc-100`, `text-sm`, `shadow-md` or `rounded-lg` compile to **nothing, silently**. No hex, `rgb()`, `oklch()` or arbitrary values (`bg-[#…]`, `text-[13px]`) in components.
4. **One primary action per view.** Two dark filled buttons means the screen has no next step. See the flowchart in [buttons.md](docs/design/buttons.md).
5. **Brand blue at most once per page**, never in the writing screen: blue there competes with the essay and the review's colors.
6. **Every control is labelled.** Icon-only buttons get `aria-label` and a Tooltip; inputs get a `<label>` or `aria-label`. A placeholder is never the label.
7. **Shortened text ends on a whole word.** Any one-line text that can run out of room (titles, prompts, rules, list rows) uses `<TruncateWords text={…} />` from `components/truncate-words.tsx`, never Tailwind's `truncate` or `text-ellipsis`, which cut mid-word ("devrait-il dev…"). It takes all the room its row gives it (`flex-1`), keeps the full text for screen readers and on hover, and falls back to a character cut only for a single word too long to fit.
8. **The essay's column holds only the writer's words.** Interface copy (status, instructions, summaries, hints) goes beside the text, in the notes column on the right or the dictionary's margin on the left, never between the title and the essay or between paragraphs. Only where there's no room beside it (phones, narrow windows) does it stack above the text.
9. **Copy: less is more.** Every string on screen, and everything the AI writes into it, uses the fewest words that still say what to do or what happened. Cut what the screen already shows (counts in the app bar, the text in a field, a button's own label), reasons nobody asked for, and sentence-ending periods on fragments ("No essays yet", not "No essays yet."). The AI's limits live in `lib/ai/review.ts` (a title under 45 characters, one or two short sentences of why, at most six changes and two strengths).
10. **A card at the words opens above them,** and below only when there's no room above. The sticky app bar counts as no room: pass `side="top"` and `collisionPadding={{ top: 64, … }}` so Base UI flips it. Above, it never covers the line being written or the lines after it. This holds for every popover anchored to text in the essay (grammar underlines, review items, the selection lookup's card on a mouse). Toolbar and app-bar triggers keep their own sides.

## Which component?

```
Does the user trigger an action?
 ├── Yes, and it navigates to another page → Next <Link> styled with buttonVariants() — buttons.md
 ├── Yes → <Button> — buttons.md
 └── No
      ├── Filtering, several values on at once (mistake categories) → ToggleGroup multiple — toggles-and-tabs.md
      ├── Exactly one view or filter at a time (Section A/B, All / Mock / Practice) → Tabs — toggles-and-tabs.md
      ├── Typing text → Input (one line) or Textarea (several) — forms.md
      ├── Extra info about something on screen → Tooltip (hover hint) or Popover (click) — overlays.md
      └── Reporting what happened → toast (Sonner) — feedback.md
```

## Product components

Built, in `app/home/` and `components/`:
- **`AppSidebar`** (`components/app-sidebar.tsx`, on shadcn's `Sidebar`, `collapsible="offcanvas"`): the logo, New essay (`outline`, because the page owns the primary action), then every essay as one flat list, most recently edited first. Don't add sections, groups, a user footer or nav items without a decision; it was cut down on purpose. On phones it's a sheet behind `SidebarTrigger`.
- **`ThemeMenu`** (`app/home/home-screen.tsx`): Light / Dark / System, a ghost icon button at the right of Home's bar. Only there: the theme is set rarely, and the writing screens' bars stay quiet. `setTheme()` and `useTheme()` from `lib/theme.ts`; never read `prefers-color-scheme` for colors directly.
- **`AccountMenu`** (`app/home/home-screen.tsx`): right of `ThemeMenu` in Home's bar, a ghost `icon-sm` user button (Tooltip "Account") opening a menu: the email (`DropdownMenuLabel`), what's left of the plan in `text-caption text-text-3`, then Sign out, which goes to sign-in. Only on Home, like the theme. Every page needs an account, so there's no signed-out state to show.
- **`SignInScreen`** (`app/sign-in/sign-in-screen.tsx`): `/sign-in?next=…` (sign-up too), a `max-w-sm` column: `text-heading` "Sign in or sign up", "Continue with Google" (`outline`) and "or" when Google is set up, then a labelled email `Input` and the primary "Email me a code". Next step: the code `Input` (`inputMode="numeric"`, `autoComplete="one-time-code"`, signs in by itself at six digits), the primary "Sign in", and `link` buttons "Send a new code" and "Use another email". From the email's link: only the email and "Sign in". Errors follow forms.md (`aria-invalid`, icon, `text-warn` sentence).
- **`ContributionGraph`** (`app/home/contribution-graph.tsx`): the year of essays, 53 weeks ending today, cells sized to the column (`rounded-cell`, five `bg-text/*` levels), hover detail in the caption. Data is one `{ date, words }` per essay, in the user's local days.
- **Essays** (`lib/essays.ts`): `useEssays()`, `useStartEssay()` (opens `/write?essay=<id>&new=1` on a blank page and saves nothing), `saveEssay()` / `removeEssay()`. An essay is saved only once its title or text has something in it (`hasWriting`), and removed again if cleared back to nothing; its draft goes too when the page closes. Starting an essay never asks anything first: no form, dialog or popover.

- **`AppBar`** (`components/app-bar.tsx`): the sticky bar on the writing screen. It spans the full window, never the essay's column, so the crumb on the left and the run details, timer and Review on the right have room. Put it outside `<main>`; `AppBarSkeleton` holds its place while a screen loads. Home keeps its own bar inside `SidebarInset`.

- **`TruncateWords`** (`components/truncate-words.tsx`): one line that ends on a whole word and "…" when it's too long (rule 7). Essay titles pass `keepLastWord`, which keeps the title's last word after the ellipsis ("Toutes les étudiants… diplôme"; a spaced "?" stays with its word) for as long as one word still fits before it: the breadcrumb, the sidebar and Home's Recent all do. In a flex row, give its row `min-w-0` and let it be the part that shrinks; put fixed parts (a "Home /" crumb, a date) beside it with `shrink-0`.

Built, in `app/write/`:
- **`EssayScreen`** (`essay-screen.tsx`): a user's essay at `/write?essay=<id>`: an "Untitled" title field (their prompt or any name, at most `TITLE_MAX` characters from `lib/essays.ts`; Enter goes to the body) and the `EssayEditor`. Its app bar holds the dictionary trigger, the crumb, the coffee-mode toggle (a ghost `icon-sm` `Coffee` button with `aria-pressed`), `EssayTimer` and Review. Coffee mode fades out everything but the text, the word count and its own button (`invisible opacity-0`, so nothing moves), and leaves the dictionary in place without its recent words (`<Dictionary quiet>`). ⌘S / Ctrl+S saves the draft and the essay list at once instead of the browser's Save page, with a "Saved" toast ("Nothing to save" before anything's written). The word count is fixed at the bottom left (`text-label text-text-3`, on `bg-bg` since text scrolls under it); with a selection it reads "N of M words", from `EssayEditor`'s `onSelectedText`. One view: a review's marks and notes go on the editable essay.
- **`EssayEditor`** (`essay-editor.tsx`): any surface where the user writes essay text. Never a `Textarea` and never a third-party editor for essay text. `value`/`defaultValue`/`onValueChange` take `Block[]` from `blocks.ts`; `placeholder` in French. Spellcheck follows `spellCheck` (the essay screen turns it off: Grammalecte does spelling); autocorrect, Grammarly and LanguageTool stay off. Enter at the end of a line starts a new line in the same paragraph; Enter again on that empty line removes it and starts a new paragraph; Enter mid-line or in an empty paragraph splits the paragraph (`pressEnter` in `blocks.ts`). Selection crosses paragraphs: ⌘A/Ctrl+A selects the whole essay, and Shift+arrows past an edge, Shift+click and dragging out of a paragraph extend into the next; copy puts a blank line between paragraphs, and typing, deleting or pasting replaces the whole selection. Its caret is its focus indicator; don't add an outline around paragraphs. `autoFocus` puts the caret back where the writer left it: screens save what `onLeave` reports with the draft (`useCaret` in `draft-store.ts`) and pass it as `defaultCaret`, so returning from Home or a reload lands on the same spot, scrolled to the middle of the window if it's off screen. With no saved caret nothing takes focus: never a guess like the end of the essay. Clicking the gap between two paragraphs puts the caret in the nearer one at the clicked x; clicking any other empty space (below the essay, its padding) leaves focus where it was. Nothing ever sends the caret to the last paragraph on its own. Paragraphs reorder three ways: drag the grip handle, tap or click it for Move up / Move down (the non-drag alternative WCAG 2.5.7 requires), or ⌘⇧↑/↓ (Ctrl+Shift on Windows); each move is one undo step and is announced to screen readers. With a mouse, handles show on hovering a paragraph, in the left margin at `sm` and up, which needs 40px of margin: put the editor in a container with `sm:px-10` (as `essay-screen.tsx` does). On touch screens only the focused paragraph has a handle (hidden ones can't catch a tap): in the left margin on tablets, in the gap above the paragraph at the right on phones. `readOnly` hides them. `renderUnderlay(block)` draws under a paragraph's text in a layer with the same metrics (text transparent, hidden from assistive tech: the review's marks); `renderAfter(block)` adds to a paragraph's block (its notes); `onTextSelect` reports caret moves, `onTextKeyDown` sees keys first (preventDefault takes them), `onValueChange`'s second argument is where the caret landed, and `handle.select(blockId, start, end?)` puts the caret somewhere and scrolls it into view. The essay is one Tab stop (the last paragraph that had focus); arrows move between paragraphs, and ↑ on the first line or ← at the very start leaves through `onExitTop` (the essay screen sends it to the title).
- **Word count** (in `essay-screen.tsx`'s app bar): `214 words`. Count words with `countWords()` only, so every screen agrees.
- **Grammar underlines** (`grammar.tsx`, server side `lib/grammar.ts`): decided in AGENTS.md (Grammar underlines). `useGrammar(blocks, enabled)` checks paragraphs 600ms after they settle and returns each one's issues; `GrammarMarks` draws them in the editor's `renderUnderlay`, `absolute inset-0` over `ReviewMarks` (same text, transparent): `underline decoration-wavy decoration-1 decoration-warn/70 underline-offset-[5px]`, one style for grammar and spelling. `GrammarTip` (one per screen, given the editor's id) shows the explanation in a non-modal `Popover` anchored to the underline (`side="top"`, flipping below near the app bar, rule 10; `w-auto max-w-72 p-2.5 text-caption`, `lang="fr"`, `initialFocus`/`finalFocus` off so the caret stays in the essay) on mouse hover after 250ms, kept open while the pointer is on it and for 200ms after it leaves, or with the caret strictly inside the words. Under the explanation, at the bottom right, "Answer": a text button, no padding (`text-caption text-text-3`, `hover:text-text`, a `::before` for its hit area; its mousedown prevented, so the caret stays). It swaps for the corrections in `font-serif` `text-ui`, joined with " · ". Never a note in the margin, never the answer in the text. The essay's `EssayEditor` gets `spellCheck={false}`: Grammalecte does spelling.
- **`EssayTimer`** (`essay-timer.tsx`, logic in `timer.ts`): decided in AGENTS.md (Timer: Edge). Put it in the `AppBar` outside coffee mode's fade and pass `quiet={coffee}`: it fades its own buttons and keeps its line, which is positioned against the bar's sticky `<header>` (nothing positioned between them). Idle, a ghost `icon-sm` timer button opens a Popover (`w-60`) with the title, `DurationWheel` (`duration-wheel.tsx`) and an `xs` Start, "Count up" at all zero. The wheels: three columns (`h-40`, rows `h-8` `text-body` tabular figures, unit in `text-label` `text-text-3`) on one `bg-sunken` `rounded-row` band; rows fade and shrink away from it on a scroll-driven animation (`duration-wheel.css`; a mask fade where unsupported), so scrolling never re-renders a row. Mouse drags write `scrollTop` with snapping off and fling on release; touch scrolls natively. Each value crossing the band plays `wheel-tick.ts`'s Web Audio click. Running, a ghost `icon-xs` Pause/Resume beside the icon, the time on the icon's tooltip, and a 2px line over the bar's bottom border (`bg-text-3`, `bg-border-strong` paused, `bg-warn` in the last 5 minutes) driven by one Web Animation per start, pause or resume, never per tick. The popover then holds the time (`text-title`, `role="timer"`), the limit or state in `text-caption` `text-text-3`, and ghost `icon-xs` Pause/Resume and Stop. Always computed from timestamps, never counted. A countdown stops at 0:00; a count-up stops after 4 hours; both say so in a toast. Screen readers hear 5 min and 1 min left, never every second. State lives in the component: leaving the essay or closing the window ends it, and nothing is saved.

- **Slash command** (`ask.tsx`): decided in AGENTS.md (Ask). `<Ask root={editorId} blocks onChange>` around the editor attaches native listeners to its root (capture phase, so Enter and ↑/↓ never reach the editor while the menu is up) and renders the menu; put `<AskLayer block={b} />` last in the editor's `renderUnderlay`. The halo is `bg-sunken` `rounded-row`, padding cancelled by `-mx-0.5`. The hint is `font-sans text-ui text-text-3` with the "/" in a `Kbd`. The menu is a non-modal `Popover` (`w-md`, `p-0`, `side="top"` `align="start"`, the app bar as collision padding, `initialFocus`/`finalFocus` off): one `text-ui text-text-2` line before asking, three skeleton rows while it waits, then a `listbox` of rows (`rounded-row`, the active one `bg-sunken`): the French in `font-serif text-body`, a `bg-sunken` `text-caption` tag when there is one, the note in `text-caption text-text-3`. An older answer stays at `opacity-45` while a new query is typed. No key hints.
- **Review** (`review.tsx`, store `review-store.ts`, AI `lib/ai/review.ts`): decided in AGENTS.md (Review: the coach). `useReviewFlow(draftId, prompt)` (`run()` reviews what's new since the last review, `run(true)` everything; nothing new is a toast with a text "Review again" action). `EssayReview` wraps the title and editor: its Layout measures where the text ends and puts `ReviewAside` in the column to the right (`--aside-left`, `--aside-width`: from 32px past the text to 16px from the window, 200–288px wide; absolute and full height so its content is `sticky top-20`), or above the essay when there's no room; the essay never moves. `ReviewAside`: while running, the trace ("Reading 2 paragraphs", "Found 3 items", never steps on a timer); then, all `text-label` and tabular: "TEF Canada · Section A · estimate from 130 words" in `text-text-3`; a `<table>` of Now/Next with the level in `text-title` and "NCLC 5–6" in `text-text-3`; the caveat in `text-text-2` when there is one; the diagnosis in `text-ui` `text-text-2`; a skills `<table>` (name, level in its own column, `text-warn` below the next level, "—" for unclear, then a 4px track on A2–C1+ with the next level's line); the changes list ("4 of 6 changes to B2", rows `min-h-8` with a kind dot, done rows a check and struck through, strengths last in `text-text-2`); the focus `<table>` (number, title with detail in `text-text-3`, ×N when more than one essay). `ReviewMarks` in the editor's underlay: each open item's words underlined `decoration-[1.5px]` in its kind's color (fix `warn`, better `c-prep`, clarify dashed `text-3`, keep `ok`), the open one tinted. `ReviewPending` after each paragraph: a 6px `bg-border-strong` dot in the gutter while it has text the review hasn't seen. The item card: a non-modal `Popover` (`w-80`, `side="top"` with the app bar as collision padding, rule 10, `initialFocus`/`finalFocus` off) with the kind in `text-caption`, the point in `text-ui`, why in `text-text-2`, the AI's feedback after a Check, and one text action at the bottom right: "Check" when the words (or, with no answer, the paragraph) changed, else "Answer", which shows the answer in `font-serif` `text-ui`. `useReviewText()` on the editor: the caret in an item's words opens its card, Escape closes it until the caret leaves them. AI text marks French with `*asterisks*`, set in Literata italic.
